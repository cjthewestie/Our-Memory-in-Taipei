# 張雨生 Tom Chang (Chang Yu-sheng)

張雨生 (born 7 June 1966 in 馬公, Penghu; died 12 November 1997) was a Taiwanese singer, songwriter and record producer, nicknamed 小寶 by fans. His family moved to Taichung when he was about nine, and he went on to study diplomacy at National Chengchi University (政大外交系). He sang lead in the rock band Metal Kids, which won the group prize and the best-vocalist prize at the first national 熱門音樂大賽 in 1988. He recorded for 飛碟唱片 UFO Records from 1988 to 1995 and then for 豐華唱片 Forward Music, where in 1996 he produced 張惠妹's debut album 《姊妹》. His recording career lasted under ten years. It ended when he crashed his car in 淡水 on 20 October 1997, four days after 《口是心非》 came out. He died 24 days later at 31. That album won him his only Golden Melody Award, and in 2017 the 28th Golden Melody Awards gave him a posthumous Special Contribution Award. He is in this repo because the page plays his first hit, `{ title: '我的未來不是夢', artist: '張雨生', year: 1988 }`, the third entry in the `'1980s'` list of `YT_VIDEOS` in `music.js` (upload on the 滾石唱片 ROCK RECORDS channel, `start: 100`), heard in the 1985–1999 Ximending chapter.

## Song by song

### 1. 我的未來不是夢 (1988)

The song that made him. It was not on a solo album: UFO placed it on the B side of the label compilation 《六個朋友》 in 1988, and it broke through when 黑松沙士 (HeySong Sarsaparilla) used it in a television advert that played constantly that year. The words are by 陳家麗 and the music by 翁孝良, the producer who signed him. Musically it is a bright, mid-tempo pop-rock song with band backing and a synthesiser-heavy late-80s arrangement. The verses sit in a speaking register and build into a chorus where his high, clear tenor opens up. That sound became his trademark. The lyric speaks to a young person who keeps going through effort and doubt. The title line, 「我的未來不是夢」, works as a slogan, which is why it travelled so well from advert to school stage. Sources treat it as the song that crossed every generation. It is the obvious song for the **1985–1999** chapter: a 1988 Ximending sound, the optimism of the years around the lifting of martial law, and it is already the page's 1980s pick.

### 2. 天天想你 (1988)

The title track of his first solo album 《天天想你》, released by UFO in November 1988. Lyrics are by 陳樂融 and music by 陳志遠, the writing team behind much of UFO's late-80s output. The album reportedly sold about 350,000 copies, and he was named the top new singer of 1988. The song is a romantic ballad, slower and gentler than 我的未來不是夢, with a keyboard-and-strings arrangement typical of 陳志遠. The vocal stays mostly in the middle of his range and saves the high notes for the refrain, so the restraint is what makes it work. The lyric is simple devotion: thinking of someone every day. Reports note covers by 五月天, 林宥嘉 and 陳綺貞, which kept it alive for younger listeners. It belongs to the **1985–1999** chapter as the love song of 1988–89 Taipei. It is a strong alternative for the 1980s list, since it is a solo-album single and not an advert song.

### 3. 和天一樣高 (1988)

Also on 《天天想你》 (1988). Taiwanese news reports (TVBS, 風傳媒) say it served as the theme of the Air Force film 「我的名字叫作空軍」, made for the 1988 814 Air Force Day commemoration. I could not confirm the lyricist and composer, so the table leaves them open. The reports place it among his early uplifting songs: an anthem-like arrangement with a broad chorus and a vocal line written to show off his upper range. The lyric is about aspiration and reaching beyond limits; I paraphrase rather than quote, and the title says it. Its link to a military commemoration ties it to a very specific late-martial-law Taiwan, when national service and patriotic media were part of ordinary youth. Best fit is the **1985–1999** chapter, as a background or alternate track and not a headline song. Most visitors under forty will not know it.

### 4. 想念我 (1989)

The title single of his second album, released by UFO in July 1989 (Wikipedia gives 17 July; KKBOX lists 1 July). He graduated from 政大 that June and entered military service in August, so the album was his last release before conscription. 陳樂融 wrote the lyrics and 陳志遠 composed and arranged it, the same team as 天天想你. The album reportedly sold more than 300,000 copies and took his popularity to its peak. The song is a mid-tempo pop ballad with a polished studio arrangement. Its lyric turns the usual love-song stance around: the singer asks to be missed instead of saying he misses someone, which fits a singer about to disappear into the army for two years. His tenor is bright and youthful here, before the grit of his 1990s rock work. It fits the **1985–1999** chapter as the closing note of the 1980s.

### 5. 沒有煙抽的日子 (1989)

Also on 《想念我》 (1989). The lyric is a 1989 poem by 王丹, the student leader of the Tiananmen protests that year. 張雨生 read it after it reached Taiwan, was moved, and set it to music. One source credits 陳志遠 with the arrangement. It was his first major composition on record and the first sign that he wanted to be more than an interpreter. The sources do not describe it in detail. It is generally remembered as a weighty, slow-building song that is darker than his singles, carried by a voice that grows more forceful as it goes. The poem's themes are deprivation, waiting and holding on to conviction in hard times. It is a political song released with little fanfare by a pop idol. For the page it belongs to the **1985–1999** chapter as a marker of 1989, a year when Taipei watched events across the strait closely. It is better suited to a caption than to a chorus clip.

### 6. 永遠不回頭 (1989)

The theme song of 《七匹狼》 (Seven Wolves), the 1989 film directed by 朱延平. UFO released the soundtrack before the film opened, which reports call a first for its time. The lyrics are by 陳樂融 and the music and arrangement by 陳志遠. It is sung as a group by 王傑, 張雨生, 邰正宵 and 姚可傑 of 東方快車, with backing from 星星月亮太陽. In the film's closing concert scene 王傑 leads it. Musically it is a rousing rock anthem with lines passed between singers. 張雨生's high, piercing tenor stands out against 王傑's huskier voice. The lyric is a vow to keep going and never look back, a brotherhood song for young men. It has stayed a karaoke group number ever since. It fits the **1985–1999** chapter, a Ximending cinema-and-cassette memory of 1989. Because it is a group performance, it shows 張雨生 as one voice among several.

### 7. 帶我去月球 (1992)

The title song of 《張雨生創作專輯》, usually called 帶我去月球, released by UFO on 24 February 1992. It was his first album after military service and the first he wrote entirely himself. KKBOX and Wikipedia credit 張雨生 with words and music. Baidu Baike credits 黃大煒 with arrangement and programming, but I could not confirm that. He recorded the album in the United States. Reports describe the single as full of electronic effects and off-beat rhythms, with a mid-song violin passage he played himself, which is remembered as a classic bridge. The lyric uses escape to the moon to voice worry about pollution and the state of the Earth, and it is read as his social-critique signature. Sales were about 150,000, well below the first two albums, but the record set up everything that followed. It was revived in 2017 for the film 《一路玩到掛》 and gave its name to the 2022 Taipei Music Center exhibition 「想你到月球」. Chapter: **1985–1999**, early-90s Taipei.

### 8. 我呼吸我感覺我存在 (1992)

The second lead single of 《張雨生創作專輯》 (1992). Sources describe the whole album as his own words and music, but I did not find a song-level credit. From descriptions of the album, the song is a guitar-driven rock track and the most direct sign of the hard-rock singer he had been in Metal Kids. It pushes his voice to its upper limit with long sustained high notes. The title is a statement of existence (breathing, feeling, being alive), and the lyric deals with identity and self-assertion for a young adult just out of the army. No chart or award data turned up. It matters less as a hit than as evidence of the direction he took after 1992: a pop star who wanted to be a rock auteur. It fits the **1985–1999** chapter as the louder side of early-90s Taipei, closer to the live houses than to the department-store speakers.

### 9. 大海 (1992)

The title track of 《大海》, released late in 1992 (Wikipedia gives 30 November; TVBS says December). He did not write it: the lyrics are by 陳大力 and the music by 陳大力 and 陳秀男, a former member of the folk group 木吉他. The album passed six-platinum according to TVBS, earned him a Golden Melody nomination for best male vocalist, and the song is his most enduring karaoke standard. It is a big power ballad. The verses are soft and nearly spoken over keyboards and strings, and the chorus climbs into a long, held high register, the part every karaoke singer attempts. The sea is the image of a lost love that the singer calls back to, so the song is about memory and longing more than romance. Later covers include 鍾鎮濤, the Japanese band JAYWALK and 楊培安. The research README already lists it as a 1990s candidate. Chapter: **1985–1999**. It is the strongest 1990s choice from his catalogue.

### 10. 一天到晚游泳的魚 (1993)

The title song of 《一天到晚游泳的魚》, released by UFO on 24 August 1993. Several sources (Baidu Baike, Mojim, PH-Sea) credit 許常德 with the lyrics and 陳復明 with the music, with arrangement by 陳志遠; producers are listed as 陳復明 and 張雨生. The song is a reflective ballad built around one image: a fish that swims all day without rest, standing for someone who keeps loving and moving without being understood. The arrangement is softer than his self-produced rock, with piano and strings, and the vocal reaches for the high register only at the emotional peak. Reports say it did well, keeping his commercial run going after 大海, though I found no sales figure for it. The Penghu tourism site PH-Sea hosts the video, a reminder that he was a Penghu islander. It fits the **1985–1999** chapter as the 1993 follow-up to 大海, a song many Taipei listeners of that age still sing.

### 11. 自由歌 (1994)

One of two new songs on 《自由歌》, a best-of released by UFO on 23 January 1994 with thirteen earlier hits (the other new song is 愛從不輕易的來). The Taiwan Popular Music Database and other credit sources list 張雨生 as lyricist, composer and producer, 王繼康 as arranger and 江建民 on guitar. It is an upbeat, guitar-led rock song. The arrangement is fuller and more band-based than the late-80s singles, and the vocal moves between a relaxed mid-range and a ringing chorus. The theme is freedom in a broad sense: personal release more than politics, though it lands in a Taiwan that had only recently gained an open press and elections. An official full-length MV exists on YouTube. It fits the **1985–1999** chapter as the mid-90s summing-up of his first career.

### 12. 後知後覺 (1994)

Track 9 of 《卡拉OK·台北·我》, released by UFO in September 1994 (sources give the 5th and the 7th). The album is all his own words and music, Taiwan's longest-running pop album by duration, and the start of his work with keyboardist and later arranger 櫻井弘二. Reports describe it as stylistically ahead of its time and commercially disappointing. 後知後覺 is the album's tender exception. It is a short ballad of about three and a half minutes, unlike the long experiments around it, and it lets the voice sit close and quiet. The title means "realising too late", and the lyric is about understanding a relationship only after it is gone. A blogger noted that it resembles Dolores Keane's "Storm In My Heart". The resemblance is that writer's opinion, not an established fact. At the 2017 Golden Melody Awards, 張惠妹 sang it in his memory. Chapter: **1985–1999**.

### 13. 我期待 (1994)

Track 12 and the lead single of 《卡拉OK·台北·我》 (1994), credited to 張雨生 for words and music in the Taiwan Popular Music Database. It runs close to six minutes (5:50 to 5:58 depending on the source). It is a slow-building rock ballad with a long arc: a hushed opening, a gradual band entry and a climax where his voice is at full stretch. The lyric is a quiet hope for a love or future that has not arrived yet, close in spirit to 我的未來不是夢 but adult and uncertain where the 1988 song was sure. In 1998, after his death, a duet version was made by joining his recorded vocal with 陶晶瑩's for the tribute album 《想念雨生》, and many listeners know that version best. The 2022 exhibition had the band 夜貓組 rework it. The album title names Taipei, so this is the most literally Taipei song in his catalogue. Chapter: **1985–1999**, the mid-90s city.

### 14. 還是朋友 (1995)

The title song of 《還是朋友》, released 27 March 1995 according to Baidu Baike. Reports say it was his last album for UFO: as Warner moved to take over the label, his contract ran out and he did not renew. The Taiwan Popular Music Database credits 吳大衛 with both words and music, and 涂惠源 and 徐昌德 with the arrangement. It is a mainstream mid-tempo pop ballad, more radio-friendly than 《卡拉OK·台北·我》, with a gentle, conversational vocal. The lyric is about two former lovers trying to stay friends. The song was nominated for Song of the Year at the 7th Golden Melody Awards in 1996; 葉啟田's 蝴蝶夢飛 won. Critics regarded the album as ordinary next to his earlier work, but the single is still well known. Chapter: **1985–1999**, mid-90s, as the end of the UFO years.

### 15. 口是心非 (1997)

The title song of his final album, released on 16 October 1997. Four days later, early on 20 October, he crashed his car in 淡水. He died on 12 November without regaining consciousness. Several credit sources list 張雨生 for words and music and 櫻井弘二 (Koji Sakurai) for the arrangement. 櫻井 called it the arrangement he was most proud of. He wrote, sang, produced and played on the whole album, which many musicians consider his most mature work. The song is a dramatic rock ballad with a long build, layered guitars and keyboards, and a vocal that moves between falsetto and full chest voice. The title means "saying one thing and meaning another", and the lyric deals with contradiction and self-deception in love, with a literary edge. The album won Best Pop Vocal Album at the Golden Melody Awards, his only win. Chapter: **1985–1999**, the close of the decade and of his life.

## Patterns across the 15

- **Two careers in nine years.** The 1988–89 songs (1–6) are other writers' material for a clean-cut idol. From 1992 (7, 8, 11–13, 15) he writes and produces his own. The hired-writer hits (大海, 一天到晚游泳的魚, 還是朋友) came out alongside the self-written albums and carried them commercially.
- **Recurring collaborators.** 陳樂融 (lyrics) and 陳志遠 (music, arrangement) shaped the UFO sound of 1988–89 and returned as arranger in 1993. 翁孝良 signed him and wrote the first hit. 櫻井弘二 runs from keyboardist in 1994 to arranger of 《口是心非》. 吳大衛 appears as writer of 還是朋友 and co-producer in 1993.
- **Themes.** Youthful determination (1, 3, 6, 11, 13), longing and lost love (2, 4, 9, 10, 12, 14), and a strand of social and political conscience unusual for a pop idol (5, 7, 8). The late songs turn inward, to self-deception and hindsight (12, 15).
- **Sound.** Synth-pop and studio ballads with strings (1988–89), then electronics, off-beat rhythms and his own violin (1992), long-form art rock that sold poorly (1994), and a dense, guitar-and-keyboard rock sound on the final album (1997).
- **Voice.** The constant is a very high, clean tenor, which is why 大海 and 我期待 are karaoke challenges. Early recordings are bright and smooth. From 1992 he uses more grit, longer sustained notes and, by 1997, deliberate shifts between falsetto and chest voice.
- **Wider legacy.** As producer of 張惠妹's debut 《姊妹》 (1996), reported at 1.21 million copies, he shaped the dominant Taiwanese pop voice of the next decade.

## For the page

- **1980s list (`music.js`, `'1980s'`)**: keep 我的未來不是夢. It is the right song, already wired, and the 1988 advert hit is the year-defining memory. 天天想你 (1988) or 想念我 (1989) could stand in if the list ever wants a second 張雨生 track, but one per singer is better for the list's variety.
- **1990s list (`'1990s'`)**: 大海 (1992) is the strongest addition from this catalogue and is already in `research/music/README.md`. 一天到晚游泳的魚 (1993) is the runner-up. 口是心非 (1997) suits a closing-the-decade moment, but its context is his death, so present it with care.
- **Chorus candidates**: in 我的未來不是夢 the title line arrives as the chorus after two verses and a pre-chorus lift; the page's `start: 100` already points at that point in the 滾石 upload. In 大海 the chorus is the held high passage after the soft opening verse; in 我期待 the payoff is late, in the last third of a long song, so a chorus jump matters more there. Any new `start` must be read off the specific upload, as the `music.js` comments describe; none is proposed here.
- **Chapter fit**: every song here belongs to the 1985–1999 Ximending chapter. He died in 1997, so nothing of his own fits 2000–2019 or 2020–. The posthumous echoes (the 2017 award, the 2022 exhibition) could be caption material for later chapters at most.
- **Licensing**: see `research/music/README.md`. The 1988–95 masters are UFO catalogue now associated with Warner. The music.js upload sits on the 滾石唱片 ROCK RECORDS channel, so confirm the current master holder before asking anyone. Nothing goes in the repository as audio; the page only embeds YouTube.
