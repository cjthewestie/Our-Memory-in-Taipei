# 任賢齊 Richie Jen

任賢齊 Richie Jen (also romanised Richie Ren) is a Taiwanese singer and actor who has been recording since December 1990, when his debut studio album 再問一次 (*Ask Again*) came out. Two more albums followed in 1991 (冷漠與溫柔 *Cold & Tender*, 飛向自己的天空 *Fly to My Own Sky*), then a long quiet spell: press accounts describe him as "frozen" by his record company for years and, at one point, on Rock Records' list of artists to be let go, until the producer 小蟲 kept him and made the 1996 album 心太軟. From that record through the mid-2000s he was a Rock Records 滾石唱片 star; *Old Place* (老地方, 2006) is variously listed under Rock Records or EMI, and his most recent studio album, 在路上 *On My Way* (2023), is on Media Asia Music and B'in Music. Alongside the records he played the lead in the 1999–2000 television 笑傲江湖 and sang the theme songs of the 1998 神鵰俠侶 series. He is in this repository because `music.js` plays his song in the 1990s list: `{ title: '心太軟', artist: '任賢齊', year: 1996, channel: '滾石唱片 ROCK RECORDS', fallback: true, start: 29 }`, the second (fallback) entry after 張學友's 吻別, starting at 29 s because the song opens on its chorus.

## Song by song

### 1. 依靠

The sources consulted agree on two things only: that 依靠 is one of the songs most often listed among his signature works (Baidu Baike's list of representative works and the Cantonese Wikipedia both name it), and that 小蟲 wrote it, since 小蟲 himself recalls "when I wrote 依靠" in a recent interview about their new song 常來. Its release year and album could not be confirmed: it is not on the 1996 心太軟 tracklist, so it sits outside that record. Musically it belongs to the slow, sincere 小蟲 ballad mould that 心太軟 made famous: a plain, warm melody carried by the voice, with the arrangement staying out of the way. The title, "something to lean on", says the theme: a man offering steadiness to someone, rather than asking for it. Because its date is unknown, place it on the page cautiously; if it predates 2000 it belongs with the 1985–1999 Ximending chapter, the era when his voice first reached the record shops along 漢中街. Treat it as a reference entry until the year is pinned down.

### 2. 心太軟

Released on 24 December 1996 as the title track of his Rock Records album, 心太軟 was written, words and music, by his producer 小蟲 (陳煥昌). By several accounts it rescued a stalled career: he had been on the label's list of artists to drop, and 小蟲 kept him. It is a mid-tempo ballad that does something unusual for the form: it opens on its chorus, which is why the page's player starts at 29 s, where 「你總是心太軟」 arrives after the upload's intro card. A lyric video credits the arrangement to Christopher Troy; that credit is not confirmed elsewhere. The voice is plain and conversational, more consoling friend than tragic lover. The lyric speaks to someone who keeps giving everything to a love that will not last, and gently tells them to let go and stop carrying it alone. Baidu Baike says it won a bronze 優秀國語歌曲 award at the 20th RTHK Top 10 Chinese Gold Songs, and repeats an anecdote that 小蟲 wrote it after a gambling trip to Las Vegas; neither was checked against a primary source. It belongs squarely in the 1985–1999 chapter: winter 1996–97, the song coming out of every Ximending record shop and night-market stall.

### 3. 對面的女孩看過來

The opening track of 愛像太平洋 (28 August 1998), and one of the album's two promoted singles. It is a cover: the Malaysian singer-songwriter 阿牛 陳慶祥 wrote and first recorded it in 1997, and Richie Jen's version the following year made it a phenomenon. The track is short (about three minutes) and bouncy, a cheerful pop shuffle with a call-and-response feel, and he plays it with a wink: a lonely guy across the street trying, comically, to catch a girl's eye. It is the opposite mood to 心太軟, and the contrast is part of why the album sold so well: it gave him a party song as well as heartbreak ballads. The album went to number one on the IFPI Taiwan chart and sold more than 1,200,000 copies in Taiwan. The song became a schoolyard and KTV chant, the kind of chorus a whole room shouts back. It is a natural fit for the 1985–1999 chapter, the late-1990s Ximending of arcades and boy-meets-girl afternoons, and it began the 任賢齊 / 阿牛 friendship that runs through songs 12 and 13.

### 4. 傷心太平洋

Track three of 愛像太平洋 and its other promoted single, 傷心太平洋 was also the closing theme of the 1998 television series 神鵰俠侶. The Chinese lyric is by 陳沒 (陳勇志); the melody comes from Japan, credited to 中島美雪 (Miyuki Nakajima); one Chinese article names the original as a song called 幸 first sung by the enka singer 小林幸子, which was not cross-checked. One compilation (滾石三十) splits both credits between 中島美雪 and 陳沒, the others give lyrics to 陳沒 and music to 中島美雪. It is a broad, sweeping ballad that builds from a restrained verse to a big, open chorus, the kind of melody that rewards a singer who can hold a long note; his vocal stays clean and earnest rather than showy. The lyric uses the ocean as a picture of a love that has drifted beyond reach, with the speaker left alone on the shore. It is the song from this album most often chosen in KTV rooms. Place it in the 1985–1999 chapter: late 1990s, the drama playing on every television, the song in every KTV booth around Ximending.

### 5. 我是一隻魚

Also on 愛像太平洋 (1998), credited on KKBOX and LINE MUSIC to 劉思銘 (lyrics) and 劉志宏 (music); one Rock Records compilation listing gives different names (王明輝 / 陳小霞), which is probably a data error but is noted below. The song is often remembered as part of the 1997 很受傷 album; the streaming listings place it on 愛像太平洋 instead. It is a soft, unhurried love song, built around a simple, singable melody and a light arrangement, one of the "gentle little tunes" that press retrospectives group with 心太軟. The lyric's conceit is in the title: the singer as a fish that can only live in the water of the beloved, an image of dependence that is sweet rather than desperate. It has stayed in his concert sets: it appears on the 1999 Hong Kong Coliseum live album and the 2002 concert album. It belongs to the 1985–1999 chapter and the same 1998 moment as songs 3, 4 and 6; on the page it would be a quieter alternative to 傷心太平洋.

### 6. 任逍遙

The opening theme of the 1998 神鵰俠侶 series and a track on 愛像太平洋, 任逍遙 was written words and music by 小蟲, with the arrangement credited to 梁伯君. KKBOX dates one release of it to May 1999, which may be a compilation. Its title puns on his surname (任 is also "to let, to allow": "free and easy, as you please"). This is his wuxia mode: a driving, heroic pop-rock song with a martial swagger in the rhythm and a chorus meant to be belted, quite unlike the tender ballads 小蟲 wrote for him elsewhere. The lyric is a swordsman's credo: love and loyalty over fame, wandering free under the sky, laughing off the world's opinions. Paired with 傷心太平洋 as the show's bookends, it tied his singing career to his acting career, and to Jin Yong's world, for the rest of the decade. It would sit in the 1985–1999 chapter; it is the most energetic option in this set for the 1990s list.

### 7. 春天花會開

The opening track of 為愛走天涯 (27 January 2000) and, according to the lyric site Mojim, the first lead single he wrote himself, composed while he was in mainland China filming 神鵰俠侶. Credits on LINE MUSIC and Mojim give both words and music to 任賢齊; KKBOX lists the music as 任賢齊 / 林輝斌, and the arranger is given as 林揮斌 (the two spellings of the second name cannot be reconciled from the sources). It is a bright, easy-going song, closer to folk-pop than to the big ballads, with an outdoor, acoustic warmth. The lyric is about patience and hope: winter passes, and the flowers come back in spring, so do not give up. The song has had a long afterlife, including a 2024 cover on a Rock Records compilation. As the first big hit of 2000, it is the natural opener for the 2000–2019 Dadaocheng chapter: spring, the 年貨大街 crowds on 迪化街, the turn of the millennium.

### 8. 天涯

Also on 為愛走天涯 (2000), and again on later compilations (影視歌全集, 2002; 情義, 2004). KKBOX credits the lyrics to 陳沒 and the music to 中島美雪, with arrangement by 蔡宗政; that credit pattern repeats 傷心太平洋's, but the Japanese original's title was not found, so treat the composer credit as "as listed by KKBOX". The song is popularly associated with his 笑傲江湖 (filmed from 1999, he played 令狐沖), but no source consulted confirms that it was that series' theme. It is a sweeping, mid-tempo ballad in his wuxia register, with a broad chorus suited to a full-voiced, open-throated delivery. The lyric is about the far horizon: a wanderer who chooses the road and the open world, and the love left behind for it. It fits the 2000–2019 chapter; on the page it would play as the 2000s answer to 傷心太平洋.

### 9. 橘子香水

Another track from 為愛走天涯 (2000); LINE MUSIC lists it as track three. Its lyricist and composer could not be confirmed from the sources consulted, nor whether it was a promoted single, which is why its "why" column is cautious. It is included because it regularly appears in fan and streaming lists of his 2000s songs and shows the lighter, more playful side of the album that also held 春天花會開 and 天涯. Its title ("orange perfume") sets up a song about a scent that brings back a past love, the sort of small-detail, everyday romance that the turn-of-the-century Mandopop of the Rock Records stable did well. Without a confirmed arrangement credit, the musical description here stays general: a pop song rather than a ballad. If it is used, it belongs to the 2000–2019 Dadaocheng chapter; it is a lower-priority choice than 春天花會開 for the page and should be verified before it is listed anywhere.

### 10. 小雪

小雪 links him to 陳昇, who (with 小蟲) was one of his earliest collaborators; a news retrospective describes the song as existing in two versions, one of them on 為愛走天涯 (2000). Its credits were not confirmed, though the connection to 陳昇 suggests he wrote it; that remains unverified. 陳昇's songs for other singers tend toward unadorned, storytelling ballads, and 小雪 is usually remembered in that vein, quieter and more personal than the hits around it. The title is both a woman's name and the solar term "Light Snow", so the song works as a portrait of someone and a picture of early winter. It is here less as a chart hit than as a marker of lineage: the 陳昇 / 小蟲 school of Rock Records songwriting that shaped his voice. It would fit the 2000–2019 chapter, but for the page it is a deep cut, not a list entry.

### 11. 飛鳥

The 2001 album 飛鳥 was released by Rock Records on 14 September 2001 with twelve tracks, and 飛鳥 appears on Baidu Baike's list of his representative works. That it is the album's title track and a single is very likely but was not confirmed, and its credits were not found. The title image, a bird in flight, continues the freedom-and-wandering theme of 任逍遙 and 天涯, now without the costume-drama frame: a grown man's song about leaving, moving on, and keeping going. Descriptions here stay qualitative: a mid-tempo, melodic song in his mature pop-ballad style, with his voice a little deeper and rounder than in 1996. It fits the 2000–2019 chapter. For the page it is a reasonable 2000s entry if the credits can be confirmed; until then, 春天花會開 is the safer choice.

### 12. 再出發

A Taiwanese-language (台語) duet with 阿牛, first released on 飛鳥 (14 September 2001), according to a Taiwanese article that traces the song. Credits were not confirmed. For a singer who made his name in Mandarin, it is a notable turn, and with 阿牛 (a Malaysian Chinese singer) singing in Taiwanese alongside him, it is a song about friendship crossing borders. As its title says, "setting out again", it is about starting over after setbacks, which suits both men's careers. It is a warm, sing-along ballad, the two voices trading lines and joining on the chorus. The same article lists it alongside 心太軟 and 傷心太平洋 as one of the songs everyone knows him for. It fits the 2000–2019 Dadaocheng chapter better than anything else in this set: a Taiwanese-language song for the old Taiwanese-speaking streets of 大稻埕 and 迪化街.

### 13. 浪花一朵朵

First released on the compilation 任賢齊 & Friends (dated 7 December 2001 by LINE MUSIC, 24 December 2001 by the English Wikipedia discography), sung by 任賢齊, 阿牛 and 光良, and reissued on many later compilations. KKBOX credits words and music to 任賢齊 and 阿牛 (some releases print 阿牛's legal name 陳慶祥; one listing credits 任賢齊 alone), with arrangement by 王豫民. It is the friendliest song in this set: an upbeat, beachy sing-along, three voices taking turns, with a chorus built for clapping. The lyric tells a friend not to sulk over small troubles; life rolls on like the waves, so smile. It became a staple of school trips, KTV parties and later children's singalongs, and it was picked for the 滾石三十 anniversary compilation. It belongs in the 2000–2019 chapter: early-2000s friendships and summer outings from Taipei to the coast.

### 14. 我是有錢人

The title song of a Rock Records EP released on 6 February 2002 (listed in the English Wikipedia discography). Credits were not confirmed, nor whether it was tied to a film or advertisement. The title, "I'm a rich man", sets up comedy: a light, jaunty pop number in which an ordinary man boasts of riches that are really love, friends and freedom, rather than money. It shows the self-mocking, everyman persona he built in the early 2000s, the same persona as 對面的女孩看過來, and it is a reminder that his hits were as often playful as tearful. Streaming platforms list 浪花一朵朵 among the EP's contents, so it gathered several of the 2001–2002 songs in one place. It fits the 2000–2019 chapter. As a page entry it is a fun option but a weak one; it is here for completeness of his peak period.

### 15. 常來

A recent song that, according to a Taiwanese entertainment report, reunites him with 小蟲, who wrote the words and music and produced it; the report frames it as following the "era-defining" 心太軟 and 依靠. The report's date, and so the song's year and album, could not be confirmed; it may belong to the 2023 album 在路上 or to a later single. 小蟲 and he are described as "teacher and friend for over thirty years", and the song's title ("come often") plays on that: a gentle invitation to keep in touch, to drop by, not to let old friendships lapse. The report says the pair want listeners to "remember my face". No arrangement details were found, so nothing is said here about its sound beyond what its writer suggests: a 小蟲 ballad in the mature, unhurried style. If its year is confirmed as 2020 or later, it is the one song in this set for the 2020– Taipei 101 chapter.

## Patterns across the 15

- **The 小蟲 thread.** 小蟲 wrote 依靠, 心太軟, 任逍遙 and 常來, and produced the albums that made him; the career's turning point is a producer refusing to drop a singer. The partnership is still active.
- **Japanese melodies, Chinese words.** 傷心太平洋 and (as credited) 天涯 pair 中島美雪 melodies with 陳沒 lyrics, in the late-1990s Mandopop habit of adapting Japanese songs.
- **Friends as collaborators.** 阿牛 wrote 對面的女孩看過來 and co-wrote 浪花一朵朵; they duet on 再出發; 光良 joins on 浪花一朵朵; 陳昇 sits behind 小雪. His catalogue is unusually sociable.
- **Two personas.** The soft-hearted consoler (心太軟, 我是一隻魚, 依靠) alternates with the cheerful everyman (對面的女孩看過來, 浪花一朵朵, 我是有錢人) and the wandering swordsman (任逍遙, 天涯, 飛鳥).
- **Screen and song.** The 1998 神鵰俠侶 themes and his 1999–2000 笑傲江湖 role made wuxia part of his musical identity; the freedom and wandering themes of 2000–2001 grew out of it.
- **From interpreter to writer.** Up to 1998 he sang other people's songs; 春天花會開 (2000) is his first self-written lead single, and by 2001 he co-writes with friends.
- **Language.** Mostly Mandarin; 再出發 is a Taiwanese-language exception.
- **Voice.** Across the period the sources describe no technical change; to the ear, the earnest, light tenor of 1996 grows rounder and lower by the 2000s. Treat this as an impression, not a sourced fact.

## For the page

- **1990s list (1985–1999 chapter):** 心太軟 stays as the fallback entry it already is. If a second 任賢齊 entry is ever wanted, 傷心太平洋 (sweeping ballad) or 對面的女孩看過來 (upbeat) are the strongest; both are on 愛像太平洋 (1998), so the master is again Rock Records.
- **2000s list (2000–2019 chapter):** 春天花會開 (2000, self-written) or 浪花一朵朵 (2001). 再出發 is the most on-theme for 大稻埕 because it is in Taiwanese. 
- **2020– chapter:** only 常來 could serve, and only once its year is confirmed.
- **Chorus candidates:** 心太軟 opens on its chorus (already used: start 29). For 傷心太平洋, the chorus arrives after a quiet first verse, and the second, fuller pass of it is the better jump-in point; for 對面的女孩看過來 and 浪花一朵朵, the chorus is the hook and comes early. Find any `start` on the actual upload, as the comment in `music.js` describes; no timestamps are proposed here.
- **Licensing:** the same rules as `research/music/README.md` apply (sync licence from the publisher, master licence from the label; nothing goes in the repository as audio). Songs 4 and 8 add a Japanese publisher for the 中島美雪 melodies, and song 3 adds 阿牛's publisher, so 心太軟 (one company, Rock, for both rights per that file) remains the easiest 任賢齊 ask.
