# 齊秦 Chyi Chin

齊秦 (Chyi Chin, also romanised Qi Qin; born 12 January 1960) is a Taiwanese singer, songwriter, producer and occasional actor, recording since 1981. He debuted on 綜一唱片 with 又見溜溜的她 (1981), became "the wolf" of Mandopop with 狼的專輯 (1985), and wrote almost all of 冬雨 (1987), his last major statement for 綜一. From 1988 he worked with his own band and studio 虹 and released through 滾石唱片 (紀念日 1989, 愛情宣言 1990, 柔情主義 1991), then moved to 東方唱片 in 1993; his mid-1990s ballads (無情的雨無情的你, 往事隨風, 夜夜夜夜) made him as big in mainland China as in Taiwan. He is in this repo because the page's 1980s list plays his 1987 song: `{ title: '大約在冬季', artist: '齊秦', year: 1987, channel: 'Qin Qi - Topic (album master, 摘金寶典)', fallback: true, start: 39 }` in `music.js`, a fallback entry in the `'1980s'` list, started just before the verse line the code comment cites so the hook arrives a few seconds later.

## Song by song

### 1. 原來的我 (1985)

Released on 狼的專輯 (綜一唱片, May 1985), the album that turned a little-known singer into a star. The melody is by 飛鳥涼 (ASKA of the Japanese duo Chage & Aska) and comes from a track on their 1981 album 熱風; 呂承明 wrote new Mandarin words. This was standard practice in 1980s Taiwanese pop, where Japanese melodies arrived with fresh Chinese lyrics, and it is a reminder that 齊秦's first hits were not all his own writing. Musically it is a mid-tempo, piano-and-synth ballad in the 1980s Japanese manner: a long, patient verse that climbs to an open, sustained chorus. His voice is already the light, high, slightly breathy tenor that would define him, without the rasp of later years. The lyric is a quiet statement of self: after drifting and pretending, the narrator wants to return to the person he used to be. That theme of keeping one's original self fits a young man's song of 1985. For the page it belongs in the 1985–1999 chapter (Ximending): the sound of a cassette in a 西門町 record shop in the year the scroll begins.

### 2. 狼 (1985)

The other pillar of 狼的專輯, with words and music by 齊秦 himself. It is the song that gave him the nickname "the wolf" (狼), which he has carried for four decades and which titled later albums (狼II) and compilations. The opening image, a wolf from the north crossing a wild plain, is one of the most recognisable openings in Mandopop; the song's hook is simply 北方的狼. The arrangement is darker and sparser than the pop of its year: a driving, minor-feeling progression, an echoing rock guitar and a vocal that swings between a held-back low register and a howling top line. The lyric is about loneliness as identity: an outsider who wanders by choice, who answers the cold wind with his own cry, and who will not explain himself. In mid-1980s Taiwan, just before martial law ended in 1987, the song was heard as a portrait of the singer and of a restless generation. It suits the 1985–1999 chapter, and as a scroll-opening song it would set a lonelier tone than the city-pride 台北的天空 already on the list.

### 3. 冬雨 (1987)

The title song of 冬雨, released 18 February 1987. Sources agree that 齊秦 wrote both words and music, and that 陳志遠 arranged the whole album and co-produced it with 黃大軍. 冬雨 is the album where he took over nearly all the songwriting (the album notes credit 張博弘 with some lyric help on one or two tracks), and it holds three of his most lasting songs: this one, 外面的世界 and 大約在冬季. 冬雨 itself is a slow ballad built on a winter-rain image: cold, persistent rain as a stand-in for a love that has gone quiet and a mood that will not lift. 陳志遠's arrangement layers synth pads and piano under the voice, the polished studio sound of late-1980s Taipei. Reception is bound up with the album's: it was the record that made him a household name across the Chinese-speaking world, and it was later the source for the 2019 film 大約在冬季. In the page's terms it belongs to the 1985–1999 chapter; its winter-rain imagery would pair well with a wet-street Ximending scene.

### 4. 外面的世界 (1987)

Also from 冬雨 (1987), again with words and music by 齊秦. He re-recorded it for 柔情主義 (February 1991), and 莫文蔚 covered it on 回蔚 (2009, arranged by 張亞東), which brought it to a new generation. The song is a gentle, mid-tempo ballad with an almost folk outline: a simple verse, a chorus that lifts, acoustic guitar and soft keyboard under a restrained vocal. The lyric speaks to someone leaving for the wider world: it acknowledges that the outside world is exciting and that it can also wear you down, and promises that the singer will still be here, waiting. The hook, 外面的世界, is the title itself. In late-1980s Taiwan and then in mainland China, as young people left home for cities and for abroad, the song became an anthem of departure and of the person left behind. It fits the 1985–1999 chapter; it also speaks to the page's broader story of people leaving and returning to Taipei.

### 5. 大約在冬季 (1987)

The song on the page. Released on 冬雨 (18 February 1987) with words and music by 齊秦. Arranger credits disagree between sources: some lyric pages and uploads name 陳志遠 (who arranged the album), others name 鮑比達; the original sleeve would settle it. A widely repeated story, from a media feature, says he wrote it in about fifteen minutes during a long-distance romance with actress 王祖賢; treat it as anecdote. The song is a slow, unhurried ballad: soft keyboard and strings, a verse sung almost conversationally, and a chorus that opens on the phrase 大約在冬季, the title as hook. The lyric is a gentle parting: the narrator asks when the other will return, and the answer, vague and hopeful, is roughly in winter. Baidu Baike and a 163.com article say it won a Hong Kong 十大中文金曲 award in 1988 (the 11th edition); a list I could see did not confirm this. Mainland sources say it was named among the 30 most popular original songs of China's reform era in 2008. He re-recorded it in 1991 (柔情主義) and in 2019 with orchestral strings for the film of the same name. It sits in the 1985–1999 chapter, where the page already places it.

### 6. 花祭 (1987)

The lead song after the title track on 狼II, his last album for 綜一. Most sources credit lyrics to 齊秦 and 黃大軍 and music to 齊秦, with the arrangement credited to 虹樂隊, his own band; one source says he first sang it in 1986, and it was the theme song of the film 芳草碧連天. Release dates disagree: one encyclopedia says 10 November 1987, another dates the album to December 1988 (possibly a reissue or a regional edition). This is the first track here where the band sound is audible: guitars, bass and drums carry the arrangement instead of studio synths, and his voice pushes harder and higher in the chorus, toward the rock-ballad style of his 滾石 years. The lyric is a requiem for a love, as the title (flower rite, or flower offering) suggests: flowers laid down for something that has ended, with grief turned into ritual. It is less known to casual listeners than 狼 or 大約在冬季 but is a fan favourite. For the page, it belongs to the 1985–1999 chapter and is the best example of the transition from his 綜一 sound to the band sound.

### 7. 思念是一種病 (1989)

From 紀念日, his first 滾石 album, released 8 September 1989; one news article says the song was written in 1988. Credits differ: Wikipedia and some lyric sites credit words and music to 虹 (his band), others credit 齊秦 alone. A news feature says he wrote it for 王祖賢; that is one outlet's claim. The original is a band-arranged rock ballad with a clear, almost conversational verse and a chorus built on the title, 思念是一種病: longing as an illness. The lyric is about the physical ache of missing someone across distance. The song had a second life in 2007, when 張震嶽 recorded a rap-inflected version with 蔡健雅; that version was a major hit and earned 張震嶽 a Golden Melody nomination for best lyricist (19th Golden Melody Awards), with credits shared between 齊秦 and 張震嶽 for the new rap lyrics. It also appears in the later 縱貫線 SUPERBAND repertoire. For the page it fits the 1985–1999 chapter in its original form, and the 2007 cover would fit the 2000–2019 chapter: the same song bridging both.

### 8. 愛情宣言 (1990)

The title song of 愛情宣言, released 24 January 1990 on 滾石唱片 (科藝百代), produced by 劉天健. The album is mostly new recordings of his older songs; 愛情宣言 is listed as the one new 1990 song, with lyrics by 齊秦 and 王念慈, music by 齊秦 and an arrangement by Keith Stuart. At about five minutes it is long for a Mandarin single. Musically it is a big-chorus rock ballad: a slow build from a reflective verse to a declarative chorus, with electric guitar and full drums, closer to Western power ballads than to the synth pop of 1985. The lyric is, as the title says, a declaration of love: a promise sustained over time, made to someone who may doubt it. In the same year he took an executive post at EMI Taiwan, so this is the record of a singer at the top of the Taipei industry. Baidu Baike says a new backing track was made in 1997 and the song was reissued digitally in 2008. For the page, it sits in the 1985–1999 chapter, at the 1990 turn.

### 9. 無情的雨無情的你 (1994)

The title song of 無情的雨無情的你, released January 1994 (sources give 12 and 22 January). Words and music by 董榮駿, a songwriter who died in a restaurant fire in 1993, before the album came out; his own collection of compositions, 紫雨, followed in February 1994. Arranger credits differ (涂惠源 or 鍾興民). 劉德華 covered the song on 愛如此神奇 (1997). This is the start of 齊秦's outside-writer ballad era: a slow, string-and-piano arrangement, and a vocal pitched high and pleading. The lyric pairs a pitiless rain with a pitiless lover; the hook is the title, 無情的雨. The writer's death gives the song a weight beyond its lyric, and in mainland China it became one of his best-known songs. For the page, it fits the 1985–1999 chapter and would make a good 1990s-list alternative to 吻別: same year, same rain-and-heartbreak ballad territory, a Taiwanese singer.

### 10. 往事隨風 (1995)

From 痛並快樂著, released September 1995 (sources give 1 and 21 September). Words by 許常德, music by 涂惠源 (also written 塗惠元), arranged by 涂惠源 and 徐德昌. The song won a place in the 1995 Top 20 of the 全球華語音樂榜中榜, and later appeared on Chyi Chin Best Collection 1994-1996 (1997). It is a mid-tempo ballad with a driving rhythm section and an anthemic, singalong chorus whose hook is the phrase 往事隨風, let the past go with the wind. The lyric is about letting go of a relationship gracefully: memory released rather than clung to. Its mix of resignation and lift made it a KTV standard in Taiwan and the mainland. 許常德 was one of the busiest lyricists of 1990s Taipei, and this song, together with 我拿什麼愛你 later, ties 齊秦 into that mainstream writing circle. For the page, it fits the 1985–1999 chapter, around the 1995 point on the scroll, and is one of the strongest 1990s-list candidates.

### 11. 夜夜夜夜 (1996)

From 絲路, released 4 December 1996. Words and music by 熊天平, arranged by 王豫民; 熊天平 released his own version on 愛情多惱河 (1997). It is probably his most-sung 1990s song: a slow ballad built on repetition (the title is the same word four times), soft verses and a chorus that climbs to a very high, sustained line, which made it a test piece for singers. 林志炫 sang it on 我是歌手 season 1 (2013) in an episode devoted to 齊秦's songs, and it has been covered many times since. The lyric is about sleepless nights spent waiting for someone who will not return, with the repetition mirroring the nights themselves; the hook is the title. The arrangement leans on piano and strings with a light rhythm track, polished and spacious. His voice here is thinner and more strained than in 1987, and the strain fits the song. For the page, it fits the 1985–1999 chapter; as a 1990s-list candidate it is the best-known 齊秦 song after 大約在冬季.

### 12. 不讓我的眼淚陪我過夜 (1996)

Also on 絲路 (December 1996), though some streaming listings and an MV upload date it to 1997 or place it on 黃金自選輯狼. Words by 白進法, music by 呂禎晃 and 張真, arranged by 涂惠源 and 徐德昌. A Baidu Baike claim that it was written for 王祖賢 is unsupported by the lyric credit and is not repeated here as fact. It is a mid-tempo pop-rock ballad, more rhythmic than 夜夜夜夜, with an electric guitar line and a chorus that lands hard on the title phrase. The lyric is a refusal to cry alone: the narrator resolves not to let tears keep him company through the night, which turns heartbreak into a kind of defiance. That small turn made it a breakup standard in KTV rooms. The 呂禎晃 and 張真 team also wrote 我拿什麼愛你 two years later, so this song begins a late-1990s partnership. For the page, it fits the 1985–1999 chapter and gives the 1990s list a less mournful option than 夜夜夜夜.

### 13. 我拿什麼愛你 (1998)

The title song of 我拿什麼愛你 (1998). The words are credited to 許常德 and 張政群 (one source writes 張政祥), the music to 呂禎晃 and 張真, the arrangement to 涂惠元; this is the team behind 往事隨風 and 不讓我的眼淚陪我過夜. Douban lists a mainland edition from 黑龍江文化音像出版社; the Taiwan label is not confirmed here. The same album includes 讓我讓你崩潰 (words 李格弟, music 齊秦) and 多少, with words by 王祖賢 and music by 劉天健. Musically the title song is a late-1990s power ballad: piano introduction, a restrained verse, and a big chorus built around the title question, what do I have to love you with. The lyric is self-doubt in love: a narrator who feels he has nothing to offer. His voice has settled into a slightly husky, lower-centred tone with the high notes used sparingly. For the page, it fits the very end of the 1985–1999 chapter (the 1998 point on the scroll).

### 14. 月亮代表我的心 (1999)

A cover, on 齊秦的世紀情歌之謎, released March 1999. The song itself dates from the early 1970s: words by 孫儀, music by 翁清溪, first sung by 陳芬蘭 and made famous by 鄧麗君. The album is a collection of century standards, and his version became one of the best-known modern recordings: it won the best music video award for overseas, Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan entries at the 6th 康佳杯 China Music Television Awards (December 2001), and one source calls it among the most successful covers of the century. Compared with 鄧麗君's light, intimate reading, his version is slower and more spacious, built on acoustic guitar and soft keyboards, with his voice breathy and close to the microphone; the familiar melody does the rest. The lyric is the simplest of love statements: the moon as proof of sincerity, the title as hook. For the page, it is a bridge between the two decades it straddles, and a link to 鄧麗君, already on the 1980s list; it fits the 1985–1999 chapter, at 1999.

### 15. 張三的歌 (2010)

From the covers album 美麗境界, released July 2010 (sources give 17 and 24 July). Words and music by 張子石; the original was sung by 李壽全 on 8又二分之一 (1 August 1986) as the theme for the film 父子關係, and it has been covered by 蔡琴, 庾澄慶 and 阿杜, among others. 張子石 wrote it out of his own life, raising two children alone after a plan to emigrate to the United States went wrong. The song is an easygoing road-song in folk-rock style: a strummed acoustic guitar, a light shuffle and a lyric about setting out with little luggage and a light heart, seeing the world and letting go of worry. The hook, a cheerful line about travel, is best left to the title. In 齊秦's hands it becomes a mature singer's look back: his voice is lower, rounder and more relaxed than in the 1980s, and he does not reach for the high notes. For the page, it is the only song here that fits the 2000–2019 chapter (Dadaocheng), and its 1986 original also ties it to Ximending.

## Patterns across the 15

- **Themes.** Loneliness and wandering (狼, 外面的世界, 張三的歌), waiting and distance (大約在冬季, 思念是一種病, 夜夜夜夜), and weather as emotion: rain and winter recur (冬雨, 大約在冬季, 無情的雨無情的你, 往事隨風's wind). Very few of his hits are happy love songs; the persona is the solitary man at a distance.
- **Who wrote them.** 1985–1990 is mostly his own writing (狼, 冬雨, 外面的世界, 大約在冬季, 花祭, 思念是一種病, 愛情宣言), with one Japanese melody at the start (原來的我, 飛鳥涼). From 1994 he sings outside writers almost entirely: 董榮駿, 許常德, 涂惠源, 熊天平, 白進法, 呂禎晃 and 張真. From 1999 he records standards (月亮代表我的心, 張三的歌).
- **Recurring collaborators.** 陳志遠 (arranger, co-producer of 冬雨); 黃大軍 (producer of 冬雨, co-lyricist of 花祭); his band 虹 (arrangements and credits around 1987–1990); 劉天健 (producer of 愛情宣言, composer on 我拿什麼愛你's album); 許常德 and the 呂禎晃 / 張真 team (往事隨風, 不讓我的眼淚陪我過夜, 我拿什麼愛你); 涂惠源 as arranger through the mid-1990s.
- **Sound evolution.** Japanese-style synth pop ballads (1985) → 陳志遠's polished keyboard-and-strings studio sound (1987) → band-driven rock ballads with guitars to the fore (花祭, 愛情宣言, 1987–1990) → string-and-piano power ballads aimed at KTV (1994–1998) → acoustic, unhurried cover versions (1999 onward), and finally orchestral re-recording (2019).
- **Voice.** A light, high, slightly breathy tenor in 1985–1987 that could switch to a howl (狼); a stronger, harder top in the 滾石 band years; high, strained climaxes in the mid-1990s ballads (夜夜夜夜); a lower, huskier, relaxed voice from 1999, using the top sparingly. This is a qualitative reading of the recordings, not a sourced measurement.
- **Reinvention by others.** Several songs gained second lives through covers: 外面的世界 (莫文蔚, 2009), 思念是一種病 (張震嶽 with 蔡健雅, 2007), 無情的雨無情的你 (劉德華, 1997), 夜夜夜夜 (熊天平, 林志炫). He himself re-recorded 外面的世界 and 大約在冬季 in 1991 and 大約在冬季 again in 2019.

## For the page

- **1980s list.** 大約在冬季 is the right 齊秦 entry and should stay. If a stronger image is wanted for the scroll's 1985 opening, 狼 (1985) is the more distinctive song; 外面的世界 (1987) is the best second choice and the most on-theme for a page about leaving and returning to a city.
- **1990s list.** Candidates in order: 夜夜夜夜 (1996), 往事隨風 (1995), 無情的雨無情的你 (1994). Each is a Taiwanese ballad from the same years as 吻別 and 心太軟, so it fits the list's karaoke character.
- **2000s and 2010s lists.** His own recordings from these decades are mostly covers; 張三的歌 (2010) is the only fit among the 15. The 2007 張震嶽 / 蔡健雅 version of 思念是一種病 is a better 2000s pick but is not a 齊秦 recording.
- **Chorus starts.** In 大約在冬季 the chorus comes after the first verse and opens on the title phrase; the existing `start: 39` already lands on the verse line just before it, as the `music.js` comment explains. In 夜夜夜夜 and 往事隨風 the chorus enters after one verse and pre-chorus and opens on the title; in 外面的世界 the title phrase opens the chorus after a quiet first verse. Find the actual second within whichever upload is used, the way the existing entries were timed; no timestamps are given here.
- **Licensing.** Nothing here is audio, and nothing gets committed as audio. The rules in `research/music/README.md` apply: a sync licence for the composition and a master licence for the recording. Note that the masters of these songs are split across 綜一 (1985–1987), 滾石 (1989–1991) and later labels, and that the 2019 re-recording of 大約在冬季 is listed on the HIM International site; confirm the current holders before writing to anyone.

## What I could not verify

- **Fetching.** No source page could be opened in full; facts come from search excerpts, and the search budget ran out before every point could be checked. Every credit below should be checked against the original sleeves.
- **大約在冬季 arranger.** 陳志遠 or 鮑比達; sources disagree.

- **花祭 / 狼II date.** 1987 (10 November) or 1988 (December), possibly different editions; whether 花祭 was also released earlier in 1986 is unclear.
- **Labels after 1993.** 東方唱片 from 1993 is from Wikipedia excerpts; the labels for 痛並快樂著, 絲路, 我拿什麼愛你, 世紀情歌之謎 and 美麗境界 were not confirmed. Debut-era label 綜一 and 1988–1991 滾石 are better supported, though sources disagree on whether he joined 滾石 in 1988 or 1989.
