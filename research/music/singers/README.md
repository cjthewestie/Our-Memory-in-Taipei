# Singers: one topic folder each, 15 songs analysed

The eight singers whose songs the page plays (`YT_VIDEOS` in `music.js`) each get a folder with a `README.md`. Each report covers 15 songs across that singer's career: a summary table, a section per song (context, musical character, lyric themes, reception, and which chapter of the page it fits), the patterns across all 15, notes for the decade lists in `music.js`, sources, and the facts that could not be verified. Nothing here is loaded by the page, and no audio is committed. Licensing for real songs is covered in `../README.md`.
