# 王芷蕾 Wang Chih-lei (Wang Zhi-lei, Jeanette Wang)

王芷蕾 (born 王玉娟, 1958) is a Taiwanese Mandarin singer. Discovered through a TTV (台視) singing contest in 1977 on the recommendation of her teacher 翁清溪, she released her first album 《情深深》 that year through the Malaysian label 大連唱片, then spent the early 1980s as the voice of TTV's prime-time drama themes, recording for 飛羚唱片 among others. In 1985 she signed to 飛碟唱片 (UFO Records), where producer 陳復明 and arranger 陳志遠 remade her as an urban pop singer over five albums (1985–1988). In 1988 she married and moved to the Washington, D.C. area, stepping away from pop; she released a gospel album in the mid-1990s and returned on 豐華唱片 (Forward Music) with 《我們的天空》 (2006) and 《仰望》 (2010). She is in this repo because the page's 1980s list in `music.js` plays her signature song: `{ title: '台北的天空', artist: '王芷蕾', year: 1985 }`, the theme of the 1985 TTV drama 《花落春猶在》 and the song most Taiwanese of a certain age hum when they think of Taipei from far away.

## Song by song

### 1. 秋水長天 (1980)
The theme of TTV's 1980 drama 《秋水長天》 (directed by 黃以功, starring 蕭芳芳 and 劉德凱). Her mentor 翁清溪 wrote the music and 梁光明 the lyric; zh Wikipedia credits this song with the big jump in her fame. It belongs to the TTV theme-song tradition: a through-composed, orchestral ballad built to open an evening drama, with the singer's clear, legato head voice carrying a long melodic line rather than a hook-driven chorus (a general description of the form; no source gives key or tempo). The lyric works with autumn-water-and-sky imagery as a figure for separation and enduring feeling, the classical register that drama themes of the time favoured. Its later life matters for the page: she re-recorded it to open her 2006 comeback album 《我們的天空》, so the song spans both ends of her career. It predates the page's first chapter by five years, but as pre-history of 1980s TV culture it sits naturally in the **1985–1999** chapter's mood: the sound of a family television set in a Taipei apartment.

### 2. 海角天涯 (1982)
Title song of the 1982 TTV drama 《海角天涯》 and of her October 1982 album on 飛羚唱片 (Discogs: catalogue 203-1; Apple Music lists 12 tracks, Discogs 11). Lyric by 樂毅, music by 駱明道; Baidu Baike notes it aired with the drama and caused a stir, and zh Wikipedia names it among her representative 飛羚-era works. Musically it is another drama theme in the string-orchestra mould, sung straight and full-voiced; a studio "live" TV performance from 1982 circulates online. The lyric takes the title's "ends of the earth" idea, the vow that distance cannot dissolve a bond, which foreshadows the exile-and-home theme she would perfect three years later in 台北的天空. Re-recorded for 《我們的天空》 (2006). Chapter fit: **1985–1999**, as the early-80s prelude to the 西門町 years.

### 3. 讓他們都知道 (1982)
The opening theme of TTV's 1982 drama 《巴黎機場》 (the ending theme, 〈我心似清泉〉, was sung by 王海玲). Lyric by 梁光明, music by 駱明道, the same composer as 海角天涯 that year, which shows how much of her early repertoire came from a small circle of TV songwriters. The title ("let them all know") points to a declaration of love made openly against the world, a common drama-theme stance. It is a ballad sung with the poised, slightly formal diction of 1980s television singers. She chose it again for the 2006 comeback album, a sign it remained among the themes her audience asked for. Chapter fit: **1985–1999**; an airport-and-Paris drama theme sung in Taipei is a small document of the early-80s fascination with going abroad, which 台北的天空 would later answer from the other side.

### 4. 秋潮向晚天 (1983)
A 1983 TTV drama theme, lyric by 夏美華 and music by 翁清溪, her mentor's second big theme for her. Several sources list it in the run 秋水長天 (1980), 天長地久 (1981), 海角天涯 (1982), 讓他們都知道 (1982), 秋潮向晚天 (1983) that earned her the "drama theme queen" label. Reports say she was nominated for the Golden Bell Awards several times in these years without winning, which context helps explain why 飛碟 later framed 1985 as a reinvention. Like 秋水長天, the lyric uses an autumn-tide-at-dusk image to carry late, unresolved feeling; the setting is orchestral and unhurried. It reappears on 《我們的天空》 (2006). Chapter fit: **1985–1999**, as the last song of the pre-pop TV period.

### 5. 台北的天空 (1985)
The song the page plays. Released March 1985 as the lead single of 《王芷蕾的天空》, her first album for 飛碟, and the theme of TTV's 《花落春猶在》 (aired 5 Feb – 15 Mar 1985). Music and production by 陳復明, arrangement by 陳志遠, lyric by 陳克華, then a medical student, who reportedly wrote it on the spot from the melody. Writers describe 陳志遠's arrangement as bringing the then-new synthesizer sound into her music, and the record as one of Mandopop's most successful image changes: from TV-theme singer to urban voice. The lyric is a homesick letter to the city: the sky over Taipei as the keeper of youth and of the person left behind, read by many as the song of the 1980s students who went abroad. The hook is the title line itself, 「台北的天空」. Sources link the album to 金鼎獎 (Golden Tripod) awards for album and vocal performance, but give 1985 and 1986 for the year. Covered by 費玉清 and others; re-recorded in 2006 with the band 六甲 as a rock version. Chapter fit: the heart of **1985–1999**, and its homesick view of the city could return in **2020–** as the voice of Taipei people abroad.

### 6. 翩翩飛起 (1985)
Title track of her second 飛碟 album (September 1985), again produced by 陳復明 with 陳志遠 arranging; lyric by 陳家麗 and 何啟弘. The album collected writers including 曹俊鴻, 鈕大可, 黃霑 (〈晚風〉) and 童安格, and the 2019 reissue was its first complete CD release from the original DAT masters. Sources tie this album and its era to her 1986 Golden Bell Award for best female singer (the award was for TTV's 《王芷蕾專輯》 programme). The title's image, "fluttering up into flight", is a lighter, freer counterpart to the homesick first album: release and lift rather than longing. Expect the polished mid-80s UFO pop ballad sound, synthesizer pads under acoustic instruments, with her voice more relaxed than on the drama themes (qualitative; no key or tempo sourced). An official MV exists on her official YouTube channel. Chapter fit: **1985–1999**.

### 7. 明天你是否依然愛我 (1985)
Lyric by 楊立德, music by 童安格. Commissioned as a Mandarin answer to the Western ballad "Will You Love Me Tomorrow", it waited at the label for a long time before 王芷蕾 was chosen to sing it first, on 《翩翩飛起》 (1985). When 童安格 recorded it himself on 《其實你不懂我的心》 (1989) it became one of the defining ballads of the decade, so many listeners do not know hers is the original. Thematically it asks the oldest love-song question, whether tonight's love will still hold tomorrow, and the title line is the hook. Her reading is gentler and more interior than the later male version, which pushes the chorus. For the page it is a good trivia point: a song the 1980s list could credit to two singers. Chapter fit: **1985–1999**.

### 8. 冷冷的夏 (1986)
Title song of her third 飛碟 album, a Mandarin adaptation of the American country song "The Lights of Albuquerque" (written by Bucky Jones, Bob McDill and Dickey Lee; Jim Glaser's version came out in April 1986). 姚若龍 wrote the Chinese lyric, and his Wikipedia entry calls it his first published lyric, written while at university; 陳志遠 arranged. Release dates differ by source (January vs 25 July 1986). The lyric turns summer cold: a love ending in the season that should be warm, a neat inversion carried by the title. The arrangement smooths the country original into Taipei studio pop, keeping the melody's easy, rolling shape. It is the start of a long career for 姚若龍, later one of Mandopop's most prolific lyricists. Chapter fit: **1985–1999**.

### 9. 交錯 (1986)
Lyric by 林雨, music by 翁孝良 (composer of 我的未來不是夢, which is also on the page). Baidu Baike dates it 25 July 1986 and places it on 《冷冷的夏》 and the compilation 《盛開》. The title, "crossing paths", frames love as a brief intersection of two lives moving in different directions, a typically urban, understated theme for the 飛碟 years. It is a less famous song than the title track, included here because it represents the 飛碟 house writers around her and because it has its own encyclopedia entry; I could not confirm it was a promoted single. Expect mid-tempo, synth-and-band pop of 1986 (qualitative). Chapter fit: **1985–1999**; it pairs well with 我的未來不是夢 as a 翁孝良 cross-reference.

### 10. 春蠶 (1987)
From 《春天的臉》 (April 1987). Lyric by 陳克華, music by 陳志遠, so the arranger behind her sound steps forward as composer, reuniting the 台北的天空 lyricist with the 台北的天空 arranger. The title image, the spring silkworm, carries the classical idea of devotion spun out until exhaustion (the silkworm spins silk until it dies), a literary image the 1980s urban ballad liked to borrow. An official lyric video sits on her YouTube channel. Vocal approach by now is fully the 飛碟-era voice: soft attack, controlled vibrato, intimate mic distance (qualitative). 《春天的臉》 has no clearly sourced lead single; this song is chosen for its credits. Chapter fit: **1985–1999**.

### 11. 你有一張不被看見的臉 (1987)
Also from 《春天的臉》. Lyric by 陳克華, music by 樓文中, arrangement by 陳志遠; an official full MV exists. The title, "you have a face that is not seen", is one of the most literary of her song titles: a portrait of a person whose inner self goes unnoticed, which fits the 1987 moment when Taiwanese pop lyrics were turning towards social observation (the year martial law ended). The same album drew on a wide circle of writers, among them 鄭華娟 (〈許多時候〉), 蔡藍欽 (〈這個世界〉), 梁弘志 (〈邊界〉) and 林子祥 with 姚若龍 (〈相思藤〉). Chapter fit: **1985–1999**, as a late-80s Taipei song of the city's anonymous faces.

### 12. 悲歡歲月 (1988)
Title song of 《悲歡歲月》 (24 June 1988), her last 飛碟 album before she left for the US. LINE MUSIC credits 陳樂融 (lyric) and 譚健常 (music) on the album's instrumental reprise; the 2006 comeback track list gives the same credits for the song itself. The album closes with an instrumental version, an unusual choice that marks the title song as its centrepiece. The theme is a look back over years of joy and sorrow, which reads, in hindsight, like a farewell. She chose it again for 《我們的天空》 (2006). Chart data for 1988 Taiwan could not be found. Chapter fit: **1985–1999**, the end of the decade for this singer.

### 13. 冷冷的風 (1988)
From 《悲歡歲月》. Lyric by 陳樂融 with 吳念真 (the screenwriter and director), music by 陳志遠; an official full MV exists on her channel. After 冷冷的夏 two years earlier, the "cold" image returns, now as wind: a love that has cooled, sung with restraint. The pairing of a pop lyricist with a film writer is typical of the late-80s 飛碟 / 新電影 crossover around Taipei. I could not confirm whether it was written for a film. Chapter fit: **1985–1999**.

### 14. 昨天再見 (2006)
Words and music by 梁弘志, who died in 2004; Sina's 2006 review of 《我們的天空》 describes it as one of his last works, and he also recorded it himself for his posthumous tribute album. It is one of the few new songs on her comeback album, which otherwise re-recorded her drama themes and 飛碟 hits (plus 〈玉卿嫂〉, credited to Eric and 陳志遠, and 〈耶穌愛你〉). The title, "goodbye to yesterday", fits both the writer's death and the singer's return after nearly twenty years. Her voice in her late forties is lower and warmer than in 1985 (qualitative). Chapter fit: **2000–2019**, as the comeback beside the 2006 rock version of 台北的天空 with 六甲.

### 15. 仰望 (2010)
Title song of 《仰望》 (7 May 2010, 豐華唱片), recorded live at the Baptist church 懷恩堂 on a Steinway piano, arranged by Ricky Ho and 櫻井弘二, produced by 陳復明, 史大為 and 古晧, so her 1985 producer returns. The album has 10 tracks by most counts: 3 gospel songs and 7 pop classics she chose herself; the title song is described as a new gospel-pop song, and the other gospel songs are 〈我知誰掌管明天〉 and 〈耶穌愛〉. The theme is looking up: faith as the answer to the homesickness and loss of her earlier songs. Lyricist and composer could not be verified. Chapter fit: **2000–2019**.

## Patterns across the 15

- **Home, distance and time.** From 海角天涯 to 台北的天空 to 悲歡歲月, the recurring subject is distance (across oceans, across years) and the place or person left behind. 仰望 moves the same longing into faith.
- **Seasons and weather as feeling.** Autumn tides and water (秋水長天, 秋潮向晚天), a cold summer and a cold wind (冷冷的夏, 冷冷的風), spring (春蠶). Her titles almost always lead with a natural image.
- **Two sound worlds.** 1980–1984: orchestral TV-drama ballads by 翁清溪 and 駱明道. 1985–1988: 陳復明 production and 陳志遠 arrangement, synthesizers alongside strings and band, urban and intimate. 2006–2010: re-recordings, a rock remake, piano-and-church-acoustic gospel.
- **Recurring collaborators.** 翁清溪 (mentor, composer), 梁光明 and 駱明道 (TV years); 陳復明 (producer 1985 and 2010), 陳志遠 (arranger, then composer), 陳克華 (台北的天空, 春蠶, 你有一張不被看見的臉), 陳樂融 (悲歡歲月, 冷冷的風); 梁弘志 from 〈邊界〉 (1987) to 昨天再見 (2006).
- **Vocal change.** The drama themes have a formal, full head voice; the 飛碟 records move the microphone closer, with softer attack and more breath; the comeback recordings are lower and warmer (qualitative, from the repertoire's character; no measured analysis).
- **Songs that outgrew her.** 明天你是否依然愛我 became 童安格's; 冷冷的夏 began as American country; 台北的天空 has many covers. Her catalogue sits at a crossroads of the decade's writers.
