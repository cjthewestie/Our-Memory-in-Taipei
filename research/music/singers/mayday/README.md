# 五月天 Mayday

五月天 is a five-piece Taipei rock band: 阿信 (lead vocals, and the band's main lyricist), 怪獸 (guitar, band leader), 瑪莎 (bass), 石頭 (guitar) and 冠佑 (drums). Four of them met at 師大附中, the senior high school in Taipei's 大安區; 冠佑 joined in 1999, just before the first album. After 李宗盛 heard a demo of 志明與春嬌, the band signed to 滾石唱片 Rock Records in June 1997, and their debut album came out on 7 July 1999. Five studio albums came out on 滾石; from the sixth, 為愛而生 (2006), they moved to 相信音樂 B'in Music, which has released their work since (English Wikipedia lists 為愛而生 under both Rock Records and B'in Music). Nine studio albums run from 1999 to 2016, followed by singles into the 2020s (the latest found for this report is 任性, 31 December 2024). This file treats the band as the "singer": 阿信's voice is the one on every record. Mayday is in this repo because the page's 2010s list plays them: the `music.js` entry is `title: '後來的我們', artist: '五月天', year: 2016`, from the official 相信音樂BinMusic channel, starting at the chorus (`start: 85`, the comment marks 「只期待 後來的你 能快樂」 at 85.5). The 2010s list in `music.js` runs from 2010 onward on the scroll, so it covers the page's Dadaocheng chapter end (2010–2019) and the Taipei 101 chapter (2020–).

## Song by song

### 1. 擁抱 (1999)

擁抱 is older than the album it made famous. According to a 2017 CNA report, the band first wrote it in 1998 for a 角頭音樂 compilation of songs about gay love, and revised it a little for 五月天第一張創作專輯, released on 7 July 1999. The debut was self-written and self-arranged throughout: twelve songs, words, music and arrangements all by the band. It sold over 300,000 copies and was nominated for Best Group at the 11th Golden Melody Awards. Musically it is a slow, plain ballad: clean guitar and bass under 阿信's young, unforced voice, with little production gloss. That directness is the point. The lyric is a quiet plea for closeness on a long night, wanting an embrace more than an explanation. The title is also its hook. The original's 1998 context (a gay-themed compilation) gives it a second life in Taipei's Pride-era memory, and fans often bring that history up. Chapter fit: **1985–1999**, the last year of the Ximending chapter, when a band of 師大附中 graduates came out of Taipei's late-90s live houses.

### 2. 志明與春嬌 (1999)

This is the song that opened the door. NOWnews reports that the band sent out demos, 李宗盛 heard 志明與春嬌 and got in touch, and they signed with 滾石唱片 in June 1997. On the debut album it became, by the same report, that year's KTV chart-topper. It is sung in Taiwanese Hokkien, unusual for a debut aimed at the Mandarin pop market, and it is light and conversational: a jangly, cheerful band arrangement with a sing-along quality, closer to a story told among friends than a showpiece. The lyric follows a young couple named 志明 and 春嬌 and a break-up told with humour rather than tragedy. The two names became shorthand in Taiwan and Hong Kong pop culture for an ordinary couple. Credits are commonly given as 阿信 for both words and music, but no consulted source confirmed this song's credit line. Chapter fit: **1985–1999**. It is the sound of a Taiwanese-language indie spirit reaching the mainstream at the end of the decade, a natural Ximending song.

### 3. 溫柔 (2000)

溫柔 is track 10 of 愛情萬歲, released on 7 July 2000 by 滾石. Discogs credits 阿信 as lyricist and composer across the album, and a Shazam credit for a live version gives the music to 阿信. 愛情萬歲 won the band its first Golden Melody Best Band award (12th GMA, the first year the band and vocal-group categories were split). Musically it is a slow-building ballad: it opens quietly around a clean guitar figure and 阿信's restrained vocal and only gradually lets the full band in, so the last chorus is much bigger than the first. The lyric is about loving someone enough to let them go, with tenderness as a form of restraint; the one-word title carries the hook. It has never left the setlist; at a 2019 concert 孫燕姿 joined the band on stage to sing it (Mirror Media). Chapter fit: **2000–2019**. It opens the Dadaocheng chapter: the first summer of the new century, a song from the year Mayday turned from new band into habit.

### 4. 人生海海 (2001)

人生海海 closes the 2001 album of the same name, released on 6 July 2001 by 滾石 (NetEase and Discogs list twelve tracks, the title song last). The only credit line found was on a later live version, which gives words and music to 阿信. The title is a Taiwanese Hokkien saying, roughly "life is as wide as the sea": things come and go, so carry on. The song turns it into a vow to live fully and stubbornly. The arrangement suits a closing track: a steady mid-tempo band song that rises into a full, chant-like chorus, written to be sung back by a crowd rather than admired. The vocal is still the bright, slightly nasal tenor of the early records, pushed harder at the top. The album has been reissued on picture-disc vinyl, a sign of its catalogue status. Its title phrase is now part of the Taiwanese vocabulary of the 2000s, printed on T-shirts and quoted in graduation speeches. Chapter fit: **2000–2019**, the early Dadaocheng years. A Taipei night-market song for people in their twenties.

### 5. 倔強 (2004)

倔強 is track 2 of 神的孩子都在跳舞, released on 5 November 2004 by 滾石, words and music by 阿信 (Wikipedia, Baidu Baike). It reappeared on the 2005 compilation 知足 just my pride 最真傑作選 and on the 2013 best-of 步步. The repo's own `research/music/README.md` calls it "the stadium sing-along; plays at every Taiwanese send-off", and that is its reputation. It is built like a rock anthem: a measured verse over driving guitars, then a wide-open chorus that a stadium can shout back. 阿信 sings it at full chest voice, and in concerts he usually hands the chorus to the crowd. The lyric is a declaration of not backing down: holding on to who you are when others say you are wrong. The one-word title is the hook. It is the song most often used in Taiwanese school, sports and graduation contexts as a statement of resolve. Chapter fit: **2000–2019**. It belongs in the 2000s list next to 七里香, and the repo's README already names it as a 2000s candidate.

### 6. 戀愛ing (2005)

戀愛ing was a new song on the 2005 compilation 知足 just my pride 最真傑作選, released on 26 August 2005 (LINE MUSIC); Baidu Baike credits 阿信 with words and music. It later reached a new audience as the opening theme of the 2011 TV drama 門當父不對, and was re-recorded as a "3DNA 追夢版" for the 2011 concert film soundtrack (KKBOX). TVBS describes it as light and bouncing, singing about the feeling of youth; HK01 notes it is a rarer, poppier style for the band. The arrangement is quick and bright, with a chorus that plays with the English "-ing" in the title, the spelling joke that gave the song its name. The lyric is pure giddiness: being in love right now, in the present tense, and everything looking new because of it. It is one of the most-requested Mayday songs in Taiwanese karaoke. Chapter fit: **2000–2019**. A mid-2000s Taipei date song, the sound of a scooter ride across town.

### 7. 知足 (2005)

知足 gave its name to the 2005 compilation 知足 just my pride 最真傑作選 (26 August 2005), where it appeared as a new song alongside the band's 滾石-era hits. KKBOX lists it on that album. The credit line was not confirmed in any source consulted; it is commonly attributed to 阿信 for both words and music. It is a gentle mid-tempo ballad, mostly acoustic in its first half, with a melody simple enough that it became a standard for buskers, school choirs and cover singers; a 2024 Liberty Times report has a station master singing 溫柔, 知足 and 突然好想你 in a row, which shows the company it keeps. The lyric is about contentment in love: knowing when to let go and being grateful for what was. The title, meaning "contentment", is the hook. Because it closed the band's 滾石 chapter (the next album moved to 相信音樂), it reads as a farewell to their first label. Chapter fit: **2000–2019**. A quiet mid-2000s Taipei song for the end of a night.

### 8. 突然好想你 (2008)

突然好想你 opens 後青春期的詩, released on 23 October 2008 by 相信音樂 (some retail listings give 28 October). English Wikipedia's track listing lists it as track 1 with 阿信 in the credit column, but the table does not separate lyricist and composer, so the full credit is marked unverified here. The album won the band its third Golden Melody Best Band award (20th GMA). It is a slow, aching ballad: sparse piano and guitar under a close, almost whispered first verse, then a chorus where 阿信's voice climbs to its strained top. The lyric is about the sudden ambush of missing someone long after the relationship has ended, and wondering how they are now; the title is the hook. It is probably the most-sung Mayday song at karaoke and has many covers (徐佳瑩 recorded one in 2015, per Marie Claire). In 2020 the band's first online concert had 李榮浩, 蕭敬騰, 毛不易 and 阿信 sing it together remotely, watched by over three million on YouTube (Taiwan News). Chapter fit: **2000–2019**. The heartbreak song of late-2000s Taipei.

### 9. 你不是真正的快樂 (2008)

Also from 後青春期的詩 (2008), 你不是真正的快樂 is credited to 阿信 for words and music and to 五月天 for the arrangement (Baidu Baike, lyrics databases). A live version from the band's 小巨蛋 D.N.A concerts keeps the same credit. It is a slow ballad built on a repeating guitar figure, with an arrangement that stays deliberately small; 阿信 sings it softly and close to the microphone for most of its length. The lyric turns outward rather than inward: it speaks to someone who smiles for everyone and is not really happy, and asks them to stop pretending. The title is also the hook. Fans often name it alongside 突然好想你 as the emotional centre of the album, and it is a frequent request in quieter parts of concerts. Chapter fit: **2000–2019**. It belongs to the late-2000s Dadaocheng years, a song for a Taipei rooftop after midnight.

### 10. 如煙 (2008)

如煙 is the long, quiet centre of 後青春期的詩 (2008). At the 20th Golden Melody Awards it and 我心中尚未崩壞的地方, both from the same album, were nominated for Best Lyricist, the most nominations the band had received up to then (TVBS). The lyric credit is 阿信's; the music credit was not confirmed from a source. It is the band at its most patient: a slow song that unfolds over a long running time, starting from a nearly bare accompaniment and growing through repeated sections into a full-band swell. The lyric is a reflection on mortality and memory: imagining life from its end, looking back at youth and at the people one would still want to see, and the sense that everything passes like smoke. The title, meaning "like smoke", is its image and its hook. Fans rank it among the band's finest lyrics even though it was never a single. Chapter fit: **2000–2019**. A song about looking back, which suits Dadaocheng's old streets.

### 11. 乾杯 (2011)

乾杯 is track 7 of 第二人生, released on 16 December 2011 by 相信音樂; English Wikipedia credits all lyrics on the album to 阿信 and this song's music to 阿信. 第二人生 won the band its fourth Golden Melody Best Band award (23rd GMA) and was certified 10x platinum in Taiwan. The album came in two editions (Now Here and No Where) with different track orders. 乾杯 is a warm mid-tempo song with a steady, almost walking pulse, led by guitar and piano, and a chorus that turns the title, "cheers" or "a toast", into a group gesture. The lyric toasts a whole lifetime: growing up, growing old, and the friends and family who were there for each part. That is why it became a staple at weddings, farewells and graduation videos in Taiwan. HK01 lists it, with 倔強, among the band's defining songs. Chapter fit: **2000–2019**, the 2010s list, a strong second Mayday option beside 後來的我們.

### 12. 傷心的人別聽慢歌 (2013)

傷心的人別聽慢歌 was everywhere in Taiwan by mid-2013 (a PTT thread named after it is dated 10 June 2013) and was collected on 步步 自選作品輯 the Best of 1999-2013 (KKBOX lists it there). Its exact first release format and its credit line were not confirmed from a consulted source. It is the band's party song: fast, four-on-the-floor dance-rock with heavy drums and guitars, a long way from the ballads above. HK01 points out the irony built into it: the title tells sad people not to listen to slow songs, and the song is a reason to dance instead of wallow. The Newslens describes it as a film and advertising theme that is all about pure joy. The lyric is a cheerful instruction to stop crying, turn the music up and get on the dance floor. The title is the hook. It became a fixed high point of the band's concerts in the 2010s. Chapter fit: **2000–2019**. The 2010s list, for a Taipei night out.

### 13. 後來的我們 (2016)

後來的我們 is track 6 of 自傳, the ninth studio album, released on 21 July 2016 by 相信音樂 and produced by the band; all lyrics are by 阿信, and this song's music is by 怪獸 (Wikipedia via search summary). It was the album's third plug song and the opening theme of the 中視/中天 TV drama 美好年代. In 2018 劉若英 used its title for her film 後來的我們, and the band re-recorded the song for it as a "眼淚未乾版" (Mirror Media). In Hit FM's 2016 annual top 100, it placed third with 84,011 votes (CNA). It is a slow ballad of piano and strings over a gentle band, with 阿信 singing low and close in the verses before the chorus opens up. The lyric looks back at a love that ended without bitterness, hoping the other person is happy now. The page's chorus line 「後來的你 能快樂」 sums it up. Chapter fit: **2000–2019**. It is already on the page in the 2010s list.

### 14. 頑固 (2016)

頑固 is track 7 of 自傳 (2016), words and music by 阿信, arranged by 張峽浩, and was the album's fourth plug song. It is easy to hear it as a grown-up reply to 倔強 (this report's reading, not a sourced claim): twelve years later, the same refusal to give in, sung by people who have now paid for it. The arrangement is a mid-tempo rock build: a reflective opening, the band entering in stages, and a broad final chorus written for stadiums, with 阿信's older, deeper voice more weathered than on 2004's anthem. The lyric is about keeping your stubborn dreams through failure and age, and accepting the scars they leave. The title, meaning "stubborn", is the hook. 自傳 went into the 28th Golden Melody Awards with the most nominations of any single Mayday album; sources consulted disagree on exactly which awards it won (see the last section). Chapter fit: **2000–2019**, or **2020–** as a song the band still closes with. It suits the Taipei 101 chapter's mood of looking back from the top.

### 15. 任性 (2024)

任性 is the band's first new single in two years, released on 31 December 2024 by 相信音樂. The label's own announcement says it was born during the #5525 回到那一天 25th-anniversary tour, written over half a year, from 穀雨 to 霜降 in the solar calendar, and that it topped several charts after release. The credit line, key and tempo were not confirmed from a source, so its musical character is described here only in outline: it is presented as a song of the tour, sung by a band twenty-five years in. The title means "wilful" or "doing as you please", which links it to the long line of stubborn-heart songs from 倔強 to 頑固. Its lyric themes are not paraphrased here because no source consulted described them. It is the newest Mayday song on this list, and the one that brings the band into the decade the page's last chapter covers. Chapter fit: **2020–**, the Taipei 101 chapter.

## Patterns across the 15

- **One writer's voice.** 阿信 writes or co-writes the lyrics of nearly every song here; the music credit moves around the band. 怪獸 composed 後來的我們; elsewhere on the albums 石頭 (一千個世紀) and 瑪莎 (諾亞方舟) take music credits. The band arranges most of its own material and produced 自傳 itself.
- **Stubbornness as a theme.** 人生海海 (2001), 倔強 (2004), 頑固 (2016) and 任性 (2024) form a clear line: the same refusal to give in, revisited roughly once a decade, each time older.
- **Looking back.** From 如煙 (2008) to 乾杯 (2011) and 後來的我們 (2016), the band increasingly writes from a point late in life looking back at youth, which is why their songs fit memory pages like this one.
- **Two registers.** Almost every era has one crowd anthem (倔強, 乾杯, 頑固) and one bedroom ballad (擁抱, 突然好想你, 後來的我們), plus an occasional all-out party song (戀愛ing, 傷心的人別聽慢歌).
- **Taiwanese Hokkien roots.** The first breakthrough (志明與春嬌) and the title of the third album (人生海海) are Taiwanese; the band's Mandarin career started from a Taiwanese-language demo.
- **Sound evolution.** The 1999 debut is plain guitar-band rock; 滾石-era albums add bigger anthems; the 相信音樂 albums widen the palette with strings, piano and dance rhythms; the 2010s ballads are fuller and more cinematic.
- **Vocal change.** 阿信's early bright, slightly nasal tenor (1999–2004) becomes lower, breathier and more weathered by 自傳 (2016); the later songs sit more in his middle range and save the top for the final chorus.
- **Label arc.** 滾石 (1997–2005) for the first five albums and the 知足 compilation; 相信音樂 from 為愛而生 (2006) onward.

## For the page

- **2010s list (current):** 後來的我們 is a good fit and stays. If a second Mayday entry is ever wanted for this list, 乾杯 (2011) is the strongest candidate: it is about a whole lifetime, which matches the page's memory theme, and its chorus is the title line, which comes back several times in the second half.
- **2000s list:** 倔強 (2004) is the obvious Mayday song for 2000–2009, and `research/music/README.md` already lists it. Its chorus is the shouted refrain after each verse; the cleanest chorus point is the first full chorus, after the first verse and pre-chorus. 突然好想你 (2008) is the ballad alternative; its chorus is the title line, first reached after a quiet opening verse.
- **1990s list:** 志明與春嬌 or 擁抱 (both 1999) would close the Ximending chapter. 擁抱's title line works as the chorus point; 志明與春嬌 is sung all the way through and has no single big chorus, so a start at the first refrain would be best.
- **2020– (scroll from 2020, still under the `2010s` key in `music.js`):** 任性 (2024) is the only post-2020 Mayday studio song on this list; no chorus point is suggested because its structure was not checked.
- **Licensing:** see `research/music/README.md`. Nothing here is committed as audio; the page embeds YouTube uploads (here, the official 相信音樂BinMusic channel). Any real use of a recording needs a master licence and a sync licence. For every song from 為愛而生 onward, the master owner is 相信音樂 B'in Music; for 1999–2005 songs, the master was on 滾石唱片 at release, so check the current holder.
