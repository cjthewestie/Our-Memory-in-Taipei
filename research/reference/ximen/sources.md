## Modelling notes

Labels: **measured** = stated by a cited source; **inferred** = read from a photo; **guess** = no evidence, placeholder.

### 中華商場 Chunghwa Market (opened 1961-04-22, demolished Oct 1992)

Sources: zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/中華商場 (measured items); photos chunghwa-03, -04, -05, -07.

- Layout: 8 joined 3-storey reinforced-concrete blocks named 忠 孝 仁 愛 信 義 和 平, north (忠孝西路) to south (愛國西路), total length 1,171 m. **measured**
- Block length about 136-146 m (1,171 m / 8 includes cross-street gaps; 1,644 units / 8 blocks / 3 floors at 2 m per unit gives about 136 m). **inferred**
- Ground-floor shop 2 m wide x 4.5 m deep; pedestrian arcade (騎樓) 3.5 m wide on both sides. **measured**
- Floors 2 and 3: unit 2 m wide x 6 m deep, plus a 3 m-wide open corridor on the east side; each unit has a small loft. **measured**
- Central staircase 7.2 m wide per block, a 4.5 m staircase at each end; toilets in the stair halls. **measured**
- Depth: ground about 4.5 + 3.5 + 3.5 = 11.5 m; upper floors about 6 + 3 = 9 m. **inferred** (arithmetic from the measured numbers)
- Floor height about 3.3-3.5 m; roof parapet at about 11 m; whole block 3 floors plus flat roof. **inferred** (chunghwa-03: 3 floors against people about 1.7 m; the old city wall was "about the height of the 2nd floor" per zh.wikipedia, **measured** but without a number)
- Road face (chunghwa-03/04): ground arcade with square piers, signboard band over each shop, fluorescent-tube soffit; floors 2-3 are open corridors behind solid concrete parapet bands (each slab a strong horizontal), square columns rising through all floors, no window bands. End walls carry a tall concrete lattice (breeze-block) panel with vertical sign 中華商場. **inferred**
- Column bay about 4 m (two shop widths). **guess**
- Bridges: 1969 武昌街 bridge joined 愛 and 信 blocks; by 1971 it ran east-west at floor-2 level through 武昌/漢口/開封街. **measured**
- Roof: flat; rooftop neon cubes (National/Panasonic, Morinaga) and water tanks. Neon box height roughly 1.5 x the block height. **inferred** (chunghwa-01, -06)
- Colours: pale grey-white concrete in the 1961 photo; green-painted bridges; night neon red/white/green. **inferred** (colour only from chunghwa-06, -08)

### 西門紅樓 Red House (built 1908, architect 近藤十郎; Class III historic site 1997)

Sources: en/zh.wikipedia (西門紅樓); photos redhouse-01 to -10.

- Parts: octagon building 八角樓 plus cruciform building 十字樓 and the open plazas around them. **measured**
- Octagon: each facet 8 m wide; red-brick Western style; steel-truss roof of 16 radial trusses like umbrella ribs, riveted; three entrances (front, back, and one straight through to the cruciform wing). **measured**
- From the 8 m facet: width across flats 19.3 m, across corners 20.9 m. **inferred** (geometry). The source also gives a floor area of 412 m2, but an 8 m octagon is only about 309 m2, so the 412 figure probably includes the porch/entrance; treat the 8 m facet as the working number. **inferred**
- Cruciform wing: brick walls 49 cm thick with equal-spaced stepped buttresses; Latin-cross roof; clear span about 15 m carried by the walls and buttresses; 12 iron columns at the crossing; a clerestory lantern (太子樓) on the ridge for ventilation. The long axis runs east-west, short axis north-south, and the crossing sits west of centre. **measured**
- Cruciform length about 45 m, cross arm about 20 m. **guess** (no source; read loosely from plan photos)
- Octagon height: 2 storeys, eaves about 8 m, ridge/dormer top about 12-13 m; floor height about 4 m. **inferred** (redhouse-03, -09: tall arched windows, 2 rows)
- Facade: red-orange brick with white (stone/plaster) bands about every 4-5 courses, arched windows on the upper row, rectangular windows below, gabled dormer over each facet at the roofline, small pent-roof canopy over the entrance. **inferred**
- Roof: octagonal hipped roof, slate/copper-green tone, with dormers. **inferred** (redhouse-02, -09)
- Signature details: banded brick, buttresses, blind arched window recesses (redhouse-08), 西門紅樓 sign across the parapet (redhouse-09, -10; 1985-99 the sign wording may differ). **inferred**
- 1985-99 state: theatre/cinema use (紅樓戲院, up to 600 seats; second-run and adult films into the 1990s). The Red House was run down and surrounded by market sheds. The 2000s fire and 2016 renovation are later. **measured** (zh.wikipedia)

### 萬年大樓 Wannien Commercial Building (built 1970-73, opened 1973-10-06)

Sources: zh.wikipedia 萬年商業大樓; photos wannien-01 to -08.

- Site of the former 國際戲院 (國際館); architect 蔡柏鋒; 200 shops at opening; green exterior; vertical neon name sign running through several floors at the corner; ice rink 萬年冰宮 in the early years; B1 food mall. **measured**
- Address: corner of 西寧南路 and 峨嵋街 (entrances 西寧門 and 峨嵋門). **measured** (photo captions)
- Floors: B1 plus about 10 above ground. Signs on the facade read "9F" for the cinema and "10F-1", "B1-3F" for other tenants. **inferred** (wannien-06 signage)
- Floor height about 3.6 m; ground floor with canopy about 5 m; total about 40 m to the roof peak. **guess** (10 x 3.6 plus ground and roof)
- Plan: wedge-shaped corner lot with a chamfered/rounded corner facing the junction; facades about 40-50 m each. **guess** (wannien-01, -03 show a roughly 70-80 degree corner)
- Facade: vertical fins (green with white stripes) every 1.0-1.5 m running the full height, small square windows between the fins, one band of ventilation louvres at the top, a continuous cream canopy/fascia band over the ground floor, roof stepping into a low hip/mansard form at the top. **inferred** (wannien-03, -06)
- Signage: round dark-green 萬年 logo about 6 m diameter at the top of the corner; red vertical 萬年商業大樓 sign about 20 m tall below it; vertical shop banners on every fin bay. **inferred**
- Colours: bright green and white (2010 and 2020 repaint, wannien-03, -06); duller grey-green in 2008/2017 (wannien-02, -07); the 1985-99 colour is not documented in these photos. **inferred**
- Ground floor: square piers about 5 m apart behind the canopy, metal shop shutters, watch/jewellery shopfronts. **inferred** (wannien-06)
