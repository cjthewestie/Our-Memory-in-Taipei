## Modelling notes

Labels: **measured** = taken from a number in a cited page; **inferred** = scaled from a photo using a known object (person about 1.7 m, scooter about 1.8 m long, door about 2.1 m); **guess** = typical value, not checked. No survey drawing was found: all lengths below are inferred or guess. Treat them as plausible +/- 15-25 %.
