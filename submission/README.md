# Demo kit: Our Memory in Taipei

What to paste into a pitch to a place operator (temple, museum, tourism office). Live page: https://cjthewestie.github.io/Our-Memory-in-Taipei/

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `pitch.md` | One-paragraph pitch (89 words) and a 3-line "what we could build for your place" list. Price is left as (unknown — needs CJ). |
| `01-square-arrival.png` | 1440x900. Where the page opens: the lantern square, the girl, the signboard 我們的台北記憶 / OUR MEMORY IN TAIPEI and the north gate to Dadaocheng. |
| `02-ximending-walking.png` | 1440x900. Walking freely in Ximending (1989) after going through the west gate. |
| `03-lantern-read-card.png` | 1440x900. A visitor's memory hanging as a lantern in Ximending, with its read card open (left in local mode, kept in the browser). |
| `04-tour-dadaocheng.png` | 1440x900, Guided tour (`?tour=1`) at scroll 0.45 (2009). Dihua Street at Lunar New Year, "Spring Festival." |
| `05-tour-closing-climb.png` | 1440x900, Guided tour at scroll 1.0. The end: the man on Taipei 101 and the closing line. |
| `06-phone-square-arrival.png` | Phone, 390x844 viewport at 2x pixel density (780x1688 file). The square on a phone, with the walk stick. |

Screenshots were taken headless (Chromium headless shell, Metal WebGL; SwiftShader was too slow to render this scene) from the `square-qa` branch served locally (2026-11-01), waiting 60 to 120 frames after each move or scroll.
