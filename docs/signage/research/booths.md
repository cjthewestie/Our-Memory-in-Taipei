# Booths, stalls, exhibition graphics and wayfinding — research for builders

Kind: research report. Written 2026-11-22 for the "Our Memory in Taipei" style-lab builders who replace the rejected name plates (kind B furniture) with posters, statues, installations and booths.

How to read the labels:

- **measured (fetched)**: I opened the page and the fact comes from its text.
- **measured (search excerpt)**: the fact comes from the search engine's excerpt of that page; the page itself was not opened (several Taiwanese news sites returned 403 or timed out).
- **inferred**: my reading of the evidence, used for every "design recipe" below. No recipe is copied from a source; hex colours and ratios are my translation into code terms.
- **guess**: flagged where used.

Rule check for every recipe: no trademarked logos or mascots (a source naming one is only evidence of the booth's *structure*), no likeness of a real person, no political content, pictures drawn in canvas code or taken from `asset/textures/posters/`.

## 1. Fan and doujin booths (開拓動漫祭 FF, 動漫節)

### 1.1 FF47 at 花博爭艷館 — scale and what is on the table
- URL: https://www.nownews.com/news/6868052
- Label: measured (fetched). 988 SP (spaces) per day, close to 1,000 circles, 3 days; circles sell 同人誌, self-made goods, illustration books, 壓克力立牌 (acrylic standees) and badges; outside the hall, a mini creator exhibition is hung on the outer wall of the ticket booth and media area.
- Design recipe (inferred): a doujin booth is a 180 x 60 cm table (half table per SP), cloth draped to the floor printed with the circle's art, a 2-tier riser of clear acrylic steps (h 8 cm, 16 cm) carrying 6-10 standees (each 10-15 cm tall, a coloured silhouette on a clear base), a badge board (40 x 60 cm cork or mesh, 5 x 6 grid of 5.8 cm circles), and a vertical X-banner (60 x 160 cm) behind the chair showing one full-height illustration with the circle name in a small block at the bottom (name height = 1/12 of banner height). Rich because the eye reads three layers: banner art far, standees mid, badges and price cards near. Palette per circle; e.g. a VTuber circle `#FF7AB6 #7AD7FF #FFFFFF #2B2B44`.

### 1.2 Fancy Frontier 20th anniversary
- URL: https://www.techbang.com/posts/103907-frontier-celebrates-its-20th-anniversary
- Label: measured (search excerpt) — only the title and that FF marked its 20th anniversary.
- Design recipe (inferred): an anniversary piece fits as a **wall of pasted posters**: 20 A2 posters in a 5 x 4 grid, each in a different year's style (flat 2000s cel colour with lens flare, 2010s pastel moe, 2020s thick-line flat), slightly rotated (±2°), with a big "20" cut from plywood standing in front, painted `#FFD23F` with a `#1B1B3A` drop edge.

### 1.3 Acrylic standee display ideas (viviON staff)
- URL: https://vivionblue.com/en/blogs/magazine/acrylicstand_2511
- Label: measured (fetched). Stacking stands at different heights; arranging stands as a colour gradient; background paper in the character's signature colour; narrative props — flower arch made from a letter holder, a theatre badge for a stage, a shrine diorama, seasonal cherry blossoms or leaves.
- Design recipe (inferred): build every fan booth as a **tiny stage**: background card in one signature colour, 3 height tiers, standees sorted by hue left to right, and one narrative prop (a flower arch = torus of small spheres, a mini stage = a box with a red curtain plane). That prop is what stops it looking like a shelf.

### 1.4 Comic-con booth design (pop-culture vendors)
- URL: https://nerdbot.com/2026/03/02/comic-con-booth-design-how-pop-culture-vendors-create-eye-catching-displays/
- Label: measured (search excerpt): "A full backdrop behind your table is the most visually impactful single element of a booth"; retractable, X-style and fabric banners are the common picks. (The page fetch failed with 403.)
- Design recipe (inferred): every booth gets a backdrop 2.0-2.4 m high spanning the booth width, printed edge to edge (illustration, not text), with the booth title as a lettered shape inside the art at the top third, never a separate plate.

### 1.5 Custom standees for conventions (Vograce)
- URL: https://vograce.com/blogs/information-hub/how-to-design-custom-standees-for-conventions
- Label: measured (search excerpt) — a guide to designing standees for conventions; details not opened.
- Design recipe (inferred): a life-size standee is a cut-out silhouette (not a rectangle) on a printed base plate 1/4 of its height deep; the base carries the small text. Draw the silhouette as a canvas path with a 6-10 px white "kiss-cut" outline, which is the tell of a real printed acrylic or foamboard standee.

### 1.6 Taipei International Comics & Animation Festival (台北國際動漫節)
- URL: https://www.thenewslens.com/article/248764
- Label: measured (search excerpt): the 14th edition, 2026-02-05 to 02-09, Nangang Exhibition Hall 1, over 100 exhibitors and about 800 booths; booths run tasks (finish a task, get a photo with maids), an original-IP award has its own booth with past works and games.
- Design recipe (inferred): an exhibitor booth (vs a doujin table) is a 3 x 3 m or 6 x 6 m shell with a raised floor (h 10 cm), a fascia ring overhead, a stamp-rally stand (a podium with 4-6 stamps and a printed card), and a corner photo backdrop. Use it for any "big event" marker that should feel like a commercial show.

Award ceremonies of that era used black, gold and spotlight cones; add two fake follow-spot cones (transparent additive cones) aimed at the column.

## 4. Installation art, festival gates and district gateways

### 4.1 Trolley Coaster, Taiwan Design Expo 2019 (Pingtung)
- URL: https://designradiant.com/111246 (also https://architizer.com/projects/taiwan-design-expo19-trolley-coaster)
- Label: measured (fetched): two elevated tracks run across the venue with shopping trolleys coasting along; built from borrowed trolleys, scaffolds, motors, tracks, wheels and canvas; track built by glove-puppet stage cart technicians; spans 6,600 m²; a focal point at the entrance that orients visitors.
- Design recipe (inferred): the template for a **gate that is a story**: take the event's everyday object (a trolley, a lantern, a school desk) and repeat it 20-40 times along a structure you walk under. Code: a scaffold grid (5 cm tubes, 2 m bays), a looping rail spline overhead, instanced objects moving on it. Palette is the object's own (galvanised `#B8BCC2`, red plastic `#E63B2E`).

### 4.2 Taipei Design Expo 2016 (World Design Capital year)
- URL: https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/feat/archives/2016/10/18/2003657391
- Label: measured (fetched): brightly coloured wayfinding; a dramatic entrance of design products on metal rods and glass surrounded by giant colour-changing inflatable spheres; a maze of small colourful houses holding 300+ works; a meat-packaging screen-print display hung on real meat hooks; a string installation where visitors tie green (trees), white (buildings) and orange (metro) strings on metal frames; a calligraphy-brush hall with dim light. Critic's note: the entrance had no significant signage to draw people in.
- Design recipe (inferred): (a) a field of 5-9 inflatable spheres (r 1-3 m) slowly cycling hue; (b) a "colour-coded string" frame for any memory visitors added to (each string = a thin tube, colours mean categories); (c) display on real hooks, i.e. hang posters from the props of the theme (meat hooks for a market, coat hooks for school). Lesson from the critic: the gate itself must carry the big signal.

### 4.3 Taipei Lantern Festival 2025 main lantern, 中山堂
- URL: https://www.cna.com.tw/news/aloc/202501020272.aspx (also https://n.yam.com/Article/20250203667339)
- Label: measured (search excerpt): a 13 m main lantern at 中山堂 plaza whose skin borrows 西門町 street graffiti, by graffiti artist 祁孟含; 2026's 西門 area was planned as an immersive light corridor with an 8 m main lantern and wrap-around LED tickers.
- Design recipe (inferred): a **lantern sculpture** = a big soft figure (invent one; no licensed character) built of lofted ellipses, skin a canvas texture of tag-style graffiti strokes (thick round-cap paths, 6 neon colours `#FF2E88 #00E5FF #FFE600 #7CFF4F #FF7A00 #8A5CFF` on `#151515`), emissive with bloom; ring it with a waist-high LED ticker band scrolling the event name and date. The name lives on the ticker, so there is no plate.

### 4.4 台北年貨大街, 迪化街
- URLs: https://smiletaiwan.cw.com.tw/article/6992 and https://news.tvbs.com.tw/life/1692451
- Label: measured (search excerpt): the street is dressed every year before New Year with hanging lanterns; 2026 used giant inflatable figures, festive installations and painted lanterns; a TVBS report mentions a huge penguin at the pier.
- Design recipe (inferred): a district gateway as a **lantern canopy**: two steel posts 6 m apart, a catenary of 30-60 red lanterns (`#D7261E` body, `#F2C14E` tassels and caps) in 3 staggered rows, a hanging cloth banner with the event in big brush lettering (char height = 1/6 span), and a giant figure at the side. Pattern the lanterns with hand-painted flower motifs from canvas (5-petal plum, 3 colours).

### 4.5 打醮 lantern poles (燈篙)
- URL: https://uptogo.com.tw/?p=533116
- Label: measured (search excerpt): before a 醮典, tall bamboo poles (燈篙) are raised in front of the temple to hang heavenly lanterns and flags; the taller the pole the wider it shines; poles and paper figures are burned after.
- Design recipe (inferred): for any temple-adjacent event marker (not a temple's own plaque, which is kind A): a 12-18 m bamboo pole (segmented cylinder, node rings every 40 cm), a lantern at the top, long vertical cloth flags (yellow `#F4C430`, red `#C1121F`, blue `#1D4E89`) fluttering, and a small paper-craft (糊紙) gate at the base in red-gold-green.

### 4.6 Taiwan Design Expo entrance traditions
- URL: https://www.bnext.com.tw/article/84745/changhua_2025_10_2 and https://wdo.org/2025-taiwan-design-expo/
- Label: measured (search excerpt): 2025 Changhua opened with a shipping-trade "源" zone using water waves and sailing boats; a 2020 Hsinchu entrance used reusable scaffolding with perforated canvas about two storeys high shaped like wind, carried on into the wayfinding (which page carries the 2020 fact is a guess). A 2022 Kaohsiung piece used 4 hanging lamps of plastic waste forming an hourglass.
- Design recipe (inferred): the scaffold-and-mesh gate: 2-storey scaffold frame, perforated banner planes rippling (vertex sine on a subdivided plane, amplitude 0.15 m), translucency 0.7 so light passes; the event graphic is printed across 3 banners so it only reads whole from the right spot.

## 5. Museum exhibition titles and section headers

### 5.1 Pentagram, "New York at Its Core" (Museum of the City of New York)
- URL: https://www.pentagram.com/work/new-york-at-its-core/story
- Label: measured (fetched): one typeface (Titling Gothic) in different weights per section; title letters carry an inline as a play on "Core"; section 1 uses wide heavy centred type like historic playbill wood type with brick-orange accents; section 2 uses condensed vertically stacked titles for the skyscraper era with taxi-yellow spots and projections on scrims; display methods: mirror acrylic, woven vinyl scrim, back-printed acrylic, rear-lit photos, edge-lit acrylic.
- Design recipe (inferred): give each photo-topic showcase its own **sub-brand**: same family, different width and accent, different material. E.g. family = wide warm serif on wood; school = condensed stacked sans on enamel; food = heavy rounded on back-lit acrylic. An inline (a 1/8-stroke-width line inside the letters) instantly makes lettering look designed.

### 5.2 LACMA, Robert Mapplethorpe title wall
- URL: https://unframed.lacma.org/node/1802
- Label: measured (search excerpt): gallery walls treated like magazine spreads; large section titles act as bold headlines and guideposts.
- Design recipe (inferred): a section header is a **wall spread**: title 1/5 of wall height, bleeding off one edge, a big photo or illustration taking 60% of the wall, a 3-column body text block at 1/40 wall height. Never a centred line on a small plate.

### 5.3 MoMA, one typeface and custom alphabets
- URLs: https://www.moma.org/explore/inside_out/2013/04/08/one-typeface-fits-all-at-moma/ and https://www.moma.org/explore/inside_out/2010/03/29/different-strokes-custom-alphabets-help-us-introduce-audiences-to-artists/ and https://www.moma.org/explore/inside_out/2013/11/01/lettering-magritte/
- Label: measured (search excerpt): MoMA's studio designs about 40 title walls a year and mostly uses one custom typeface; for some shows it draws custom alphabets to introduce the artist.
- Design recipe (inferred): for the exhibition wall header, draw **custom lettering** in canvas paths (e.g. letters built from film strips for a cinema memory, from chopsticks for food), kept to the title only; all body text stays one plain family.

### 5.4 Milwaukee Art Museum, "Color Rush" title wall
- URL: https://blog.mam.org/2013/05/24/the-design-behind-color-rush/
- Label: measured (search excerpt): the approach was to make the title big, "really big".
- Design recipe (inferred): scale is a design move: the title spans the whole wall (letter height ~0.6 of wall height), cropped by the ceiling or the corner.

### 5.5 Wellcome Collection, inclusive exhibition text
- URL: https://wellcomecollection.org/exhibition-texts
- Label: measured (search excerpt): section panels in consistent positions at the start of each section; vertically mounted section texts centred 1200-1500 mm above the floor.
- Design recipe (inferred): the one piece of plain readable text in each display sits at 1.2-1.5 m and is small; everything above 1.8 m is the designed graphic.

### 5.6 C-GRAPHIC/TAIPEI, NOKE 忠泰樂生活 (2026)
- URL: https://www.marieclaire.com.tw/lifestyle/art/96401
- Label: measured (fetched): key visual by Tetsuya Goto and Javin Mo built on the characters 「台北」; high saturation pink, orange, green, blue and yellow colliding, drawn from street signs, night markets, temples and fireworks; an open non-linear route mixing posters, books and identities; 8 Oct-9 Nov 2026.
- Design recipe (inferred): the "Taipei" palette for any exhibition wall: `#FF4FA0 #FF7A1A #18B26B #1F5BFF #FFD600` on `#F7F3EA`, colours overlapping in multiply blend; huge 「台北」 strokes broken into coloured blocks per stroke.

### 5.7 故宮 「大美不言」 key visual and showcases
- URL: https://www.cna.com.tw/news/acul/202409190309.aspx
- Label: measured (search excerpt): key visual centred on an angel sculpture from a vessel, over a background from a 洋彩 vase's twin-dragon pattern, layered with a necklace; transparent wave-shaped showcases "like a crystal palace" (designer attribution differs between sources).
- Design recipe (inferred): a lit-case display: curved transparent showcase (glass, transmission 0.9, slight blue `#DDEFFF`), object on a plinth inside, title layered over a pattern pulled from the object itself (a repeating motif at 15% opacity).

## 6. Wayfinding and street graphics (for kind B site wayfinding; kind A real plates follow the real sign)

### 6.1 justfont on Taipei MRT sign typography
- URL: https://blog.justfont.com/2013/03/mrt-some-investigation-on-inf/
- Label: measured (fetched): the standard calls for heavy 黑體 for Chinese, Helvetica for English, an adjusted 特明體 with thickened horizontals for Chinese station names and Optima Bold for English station names; when station names were switched to heavy sans at similar sizes to other text, they became hard to tell from directions; cramped spacing on some signs.
- Design recipe (inferred): hierarchy needs **contrast of style**, not just size: the place name in a different style (Ming/serif) from the directions (heavy sans), name ≥1.6x direction height, letter-spacing +5%. Applies to our gate boards.

### 6.2 Taipei metro signage standard and the font change
- URLs: https://www.metro.taipei/cp.aspx?n=9E1943CD7EEE868B and https://www.bnext.com.tw/article/80440/taipei-mrt-front-change
- Label: measured (search excerpt): Metro Taipei is moving station names to 黑體 under the latest signage guideline because even stroke weight reads better for older people; Japanese and Korean added at key stations.
- Design recipe (inferred): if a gate board must carry info, do it as a **wayfinding totem**: 0.6 x 2.4 m, coloured line bands at the top (line colours e.g. `#E3002C #0070BD #008659 #F8B61C #C48C31`), pictograms 1/6 width, then text. The colour bands are what make it look designed.

### 6.3 小招牌製造所 (2016 World Design Capital small-sign project) and 聶永真's market signs
- URLs: https://www.gvm.com.tw/article/30670 and https://www.setn.com/news/142804
- Label: measured (fetched, setn): at 大直市場, nine new signs by several designers; 聶永真 did three, mainly three-dimensional motifs meant to catch passers-by, warmer than his usual style; another designer's sign used black and gold. (search excerpt, gvm): the project's goal was to redesign 100 small signs.
- Design recipe (inferred): a designed sign is **an object**: the motif is a 3D shape (a fish, a bowl, a vegetable) that sticks out of the board, the name is lettering wrapped around or carved into the shape. Use this for any food-market showcase header.

## 8. School and commute topic

### 8.1 台灣老掛圖 (old classroom wall charts)
- URL: https://www.worldjournal.com/wj/amp/story/124760/9323940
- Label: measured (fetched): about 400 educational wall charts; hand-painted, vivid colours, more image than text for young pupils; naive yet detailed, loose perspective, cross-sections (flower, rabbit organs); stereotyped figures (mothers in qipao with pinned hair, fathers with glasses and crew cuts); subjects from national textbooks (language, maths, nature, hygiene, school life, music, rules).
- Design recipe (inferred): the school showcase header as a **hanging wall chart**: 1.0 x 1.4 m paper on a wooden top and bottom rod with a cord, flat gouache colours `#E94B3C #F6C343 #3FA34D #3B7DD8 #FDF6E3`, a big illustrated scene (classroom, flag-less playground, a cross-section of a school bag), a title in rounded bold at the top (1/10 height) with 注音 beside it, numbered labels with leader lines. Hang several, slightly curled.

### 8.2 Old School Calendar (UDNTime 2023, by Yang Fong-Ming studio)

## Do not do

What made the three rejected plates (金曲獎 purple plate on posts, 家人 FAMILY header with two camera icons, 北投公園 teal plate) fail, and what must never appear for kind (B):

- **A rectangle with a title line and a date line in one font.** Every piece needs at least two typographic styles (display lettering + a small text face) and at least 3 sizes (ratio ≥ 4:1 between biggest and smallest).
- **A thin border around flat colour.** Replace the frame with material: wood grain, enamel, acrylic edge glow, paper curl, scaffold, cloth.
- **Text as the only content.** At least 60% of the face area is picture: illustration, pattern, photo, pictogram scene, or the object itself.
- **Icons as decoration** (two camera icons flanking a word). A pictogram is used only as a composition element at real size, or as a 3D prop.
- **One height and one plane.** Each piece has depth layers: background art, a mid layer (standees, props, cut-out letters), a front layer (small text, plinth plate, price cards, floor decal), and ideally something overhead.
- **Posts holding up a board.** Mount it like the real thing: standee base, lit case, column wrap, hanging rods, scaffolding, canopy tent, lantern cable, plinth.
- **Same recipe twice.** No two markers share a format; check the list above and pick a different kind per event.
- **Nothing that says what it felt like.** Each piece answers "what did the crowd do there": wrote cards, queued, sat in the chair, lit lanterns, posed for a photo, ate. Build that action into the piece (cards, queue posts, chair, lantern, stand-here decal, steam).
- **A dated plate as the only date.** Dates are part of the composition: giant numerals cut off by the edge, a tear-off calendar page, a ticket stub, an LED ticker.
- **Generic palettes** (a single mid purple or teal). Use the palette of that event's kind and year from the recipes above.
- **Licensed characters, brand logos, real-person likenesses, political imagery.** Invent figures and use generic pictograms; name people in text only.
