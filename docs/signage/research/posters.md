# Event posters by era and kind

For: builders replacing the kind (B) plates in Our Memory in Taipei (event markers, showcase headers, gate boards, exhibition header). CJ, 2026-11-22: 「反正這些招牌 字都是要設計過的可以是海報雕像或是裝置藝術攤位 可以去做大量研究要非常豐富就對了這種無聊的招牌絕對不能出現就對了」.

## How to read this file

- Every reference was opened through web search or fetch on 2026-11-22. Labels: **measured** = the fact is stated on the page or in the search result for it; **inferred** = my reading of what the page states; **guess** = my own design proposal (all hex colours are guesses unless marked otherwise — I did not see the images, only the text describing them).
- Recipes use ratios of poster height H and width W. A standard portrait poster is W:H = 1:1.414 (A/B paper) — inferred.
- Rules carried from the brief: no trademarked logos or mascots (no Pokémon, no Riot champions, no 熊讚, no Haibao), no drawn likeness of real people (names in text are fine), no political content. Draw everything in canvas code.
- Count: 54 references, each with a URL (`grep -cE '^[0-9]+\. \*\*.*https?://' posters.md` → 54).

## 1. Award shows and music awards (金曲獎, 金馬獎)

**Era note (inferred from ref 6):** Taiwanese award posters of the early-to-mid 2000s were dense collage illustration; after about 2008 they moved to black-and-white minimalism and photographic concept art. A 2006 金曲獎 piece should therefore look busy, glossy and layered, not minimal.

1. **金曲28 (2017) main visual by 顏伯駿** — five covers, one per music carrier (vinyl, cassette, CD, digital, future), told through fashion photography: a giant disc hat for vinyl, cassette tape as hair; typeface 文鼎晶熙黑; won Red Dot and iF. https://www.inside.com.tw/article/9732-golden-melody-awards-font — measured (search summary; the page itself returned 403).
2. **Same campaign, press coverage** — "載具萬變，音樂永存" theme; the ceremony read as a music event rather than a formal state ceremony after this redesign. https://www.marieclaire.com.tw/lifestyle/issue/33164 — measured (search summary).
3. **金曲29 main visual thesis (Airiti)** — an academic study of the 29th edition's main visual (JL Design, 「眾生宣言」). https://www.airitilibrary.com/Article/Detail/a0000537-202006-202007270004-202007270004-387-398 — measured (title and abstract listing only).
4. **金曲17 (2006) — the year the game shows** — the 2006 ceremony (17th GMA) nominees list, dated 2006-06-10. No source for the 2006 poster's design was found. https://news.tvbs.com.tw/life/361856 — measured that no poster source was found; the date is from the search result.
5. **金馬63 (2026) day/night pair by 殷振豪 + Blob Club** — layered ink-wash mountain ridges; palette taken from 14 classic Chinese-language films; 「氣」 drawn as wind and mist winding between the characters 金 and 馬; two posters joined side by side make one wide screen-shaped landscape; seal-style inscriptions. https://www.bella.tw/articles/movies&culture/58995/golden-horse-63-design — measured (page fetched).
6. **金馬 poster study 2001–2021** — "after 2008, the complex collage illustration was dropped for black-and-white minimalism." https://www.airitilibrary.com/Article/Detail/P20230419001-N202304190017-00005 — measured (search summary of the abstract).
7. **金馬56 (2019) 「尋找黑馬」 by JL Design / 羅申駿** — heavy black tone, after Randal Ford's black-horse photographs. https://www.taiwannews.com.tw/zh/news/3754944 — measured (search summary).

**Recipe — "金曲獎 登上小巨蛋 2006.6.10" as a POSTER WALL or LIT CASE (guess):**
- Build a free-standing lit case 1.2 m x 2.2 m (two-sided, light box glow), or a 3 x 2 grid of pasted posters on a column wrap. Not one plate.
- Poster grid: 12 columns, margins 0.05W. Top 0.62H is a collage: a giant vinyl disc (circle, radius 0.38W, concentric grooves every 0.004W in #1A1A1A over #000, a label circle 0.12W in gold #C9A24A), a cassette reel strip curving across it like hair (two 0.05W-tall brown #5A3A22 ribbons drawn as Bézier curves), and 30-60 spark stars (4-point, #FFF6D0) — collage density is the 2000s signal (inferred from ref 6).
- Title block: 「金曲」 set huge, cap height 0.16H, in a heavy black sans (晶熙黑 stand-in: canvas `900 sans-serif` with letter-spacing -0.02em), white with a 0.004H gold outline and a soft drop shadow; 「第17屆」 vertical at the right edge in 0.035H; the date 「2006.6.10」 as a separate graphic band at 0.83H — tabular figures 0.05H on a gold ribbon. Latin line "GOLDEN MELODY AWARDS" tracked +0.3em at 0.018H.
- Venue strip at the foot (0.06H): 「台北小巨蛋」 in 0.022H reverse type on #111.
- Palette (guess): night black #0B0B10, gold #C9A24A to #F3D98B gradient, ruby #A3142B, white. What makes it rich: grooves, reel ribbon, stars, gold foil gradient, three type sizes in a 1:3:8 ratio, light-box glow.
- Second layer around it: red carpet (a 1.2 m strip), stanchions with velvet rope, a photo backdrop "step-and-repeat" wall that repeats a made-up ceremony wordmark (never a real logo) in a diagonal grid.

**Recipe — 花博 2010 marker as an INSTALLATION (guess):**
- A walk-under arch of 9 giant flowers on stems (cylinders 0.08 m, heights 3-6 m). Each flower is a ring of 6-10 petals (flattened ellipsoids) in a different colour of the "彩花" palette: magenta #E4007F, orange #F39800, yellow #FFE100, leaf green #8FC31F, sky #00A0E9 (guess). Stems lean in so the heads form a canopy; a shallow "流水" ribbon of blue glass tiles (#5BC0EB, emissive 0.2) runs under it — that's the theme's three words made physical.
- On one side, a column-wrap poster (cylinder r 0.5 m): a dancer silhouette made of petals (a figure path filled with 200 small petal ellipses, no face) leaping across a green-to-cyan gradient; 「2010 臺北國際花卉博覽會」 at 0.06H in a rounded sans; dates as a line of nine flower dots with numbers under them.
- What makes it rich: scale (you walk under it), many petal colours, the water ribbon, a poster that repeats the flowers as pattern.

## Do not do (what made the rejected plates fail)

The three rejected pieces (`docs/signage/rejected/*.png`) share one shape: a rectangle, one title line, one date line, one font, a thin border, on two posts. Any of these brings it back:

1. **One shape, flat colour.** A board that is a box with a fill. Every piece needs at least three depth layers: a structure (case, column, truss, plinth), a picture (illustration, pattern, figure), and type.
2. **One font, two lines.** Title + date in one weight and one size. Use at least three sizes in a ratio near 1:3:8, a mix of Chinese and Latin, and lettering that is part of the picture (built from beams, strings, brush, cut paper).
3. **Text centred in empty space.** Put the type on a grid (12-col or 6-col), let the largest element bleed off an edge, and fill the rest with picture.
4. **A thin outline border as the only "design".** Replace with a real frame: light box edge, torn paper edges, banner hems and grommets, a plinth moulding.
5. **Icons as decoration** (the two camera icons on 家人 FAMILY). A pictogram must be the subject drawn big, not a sticker next to the word.
6. **Same template for every event.** No two pieces may share kind (poster / statue / installation / booth), palette, or mounting.
7. **Nothing that says what it felt like.** Each piece needs one image of the moment — the crowd, the light, the gold, the lanterns, the glowing phones — not just its name.
8. **Two posts in grass.** Mount it the way the real thing is mounted: lit case, column wrap, pole banners, scaffold billboard, truss booth, plinth.
9. **Logos, mascots, faces.** Never the real event logo, a game mascot, or a real person's face — that is a trademark or likeness problem, and a stand-in drawn badly looks cheaper than an original design.
