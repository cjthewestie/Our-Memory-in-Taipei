# 西門紅樓 plaza: where each sign comes from

CJ, 2026-11-21: 「還有這些招牌都怪怪的請出搜尋真實招牌 海報跟布條」. Every sign, poster and banner in `remake/landmarks-ximen.js` is listed here with its source. Labels: **measured** = seen in the photo or read on the page that was opened; **inferred** = a reading of that evidence, or general knowledge not opened this session; **guess** = no source. Everything is redrawn on a canvas; no photo and no logo is copied. Before and after screenshots of the six viewpoints are in `shots/`.

## What was wrong before (inferred, from `shots/before-*.png`)

- Every sign was set in one modern screen typeface (PingFang) with the same thin box border; period signs were brush-written (楷書, 魏碑, 行書) or acrylic light boxes.
- Signs carried museum-caption lines no real sign has: "FONG DA COFFEE · 1956", "LIONS' PLAZA · 1979", "忠棟 · 第一棟 · 1961", "TATTOO STREET · 漢中街50巷".
- Film titles were invented (霓虹之夜, 台北假期, 少年遊, 再見黃昏, 長巷, 萬年青春), with cartoon faces.
- 中華商場 carried generic trades (唱片行, 集郵社) and generic rooftop boards (電器, 鋼筆, 味精), not shop or brand names.
- 來來百貨 carried four invented sale banners (最後倒數 among them) and a 結束營業 board; 天后宮 had an unverified couplet and a black-and-gold plaque.

## Second pass: matched by eye

CJ, 2026-11-21: 「很多沒有按照真實招牌」. The first pass drew the signs from written descriptions of the photos. In this pass S1, S3, S4, S14, S16, S20, S21, S23 and S25 were opened and looked at, and these signs were redrawn to what the photos show:

- 蜂大咖啡: white fascia with black striped blocks at both ends, a round mark at the left, heavy black block characters, the small 1956 line; a tall white upright between the two shops (S20).
- 南美咖啡: cream heavy brush characters on dark maroon with a thin cream rule (S20).
- 阿宗麵線: brown louvre grilles each side of the white board, a gold and green wave under the red brush name, a red and white striped awning (S21).
- 成都楊桃冰: wide-spaced green brush characters with the red 創於1966年 under them; the red strip with gold characters read right to left inside (S23).
- 台北天后宮: white brush name on dark red; the two tall yellow signs hang off the upper floors at each side, not on the door posts (S25).
- 萬年: the name down the building is solid red block characters, not brush or tube; the band over the door is cream with black characters (S14).
- 中華商場: the walkway banner is light cloth with dark characters; the upright boards are tall white boards with black characters; the 國際牌 tower is back on its frame, red with a white ring and the name across it, blue bands at the corners (S1, S3, S4). Its lightning-bolt mark is left out.

Third pass, the fronts (CJ, 2026-11-21: "Yes, rebuild fronts"). Rebuilt from the same photos: 蜂大 in red brick with the white panel in its second-floor window, 南美 in small white tile with a railed roof terrace (S20); 天后宮 as three storeys of dark red brick, the gate under a carved gilt eave and six lanterns, the name over a round red disc on the second floor, a balcony behind two columns on the third, a swallow-tail roof on top (S25); 萬年 in green with narrow white ribs, the name down the flat front under the oval, four tenant signs hung at the sides, a cream band over the ground floor (S14); 獅子林 in gold panels with a rounded corner tower, two dark glass strips, and the crown sign with two globe lamps on top (S16, S26, S29). Simplified, not copied: the temple's emblem is a plain red disc and its roof dragons are blocks; the 蜂大 mark is a plain hexagon; the 獅子林 crown lettering is ours. 萬年's tenant signs mix eras (萬年冰宮 early, 湯姆熊 and U2電影館 later).

## Poster pictures

The painted pictures on the 豪華 billboard, the two poster cases, the three 獅子林 posters and the 來來 poster are Midjourney images made for this scene, in `asset/textures/posters/` (`billboard`, `case-a`, `case-b`, `kids`, `army`, `denim`, `lailai`). ## Not done

- 西門紅樓 itself has no sign in the scene; the real one is a small stone plaque over the door (S27).
- 國家文化記憶庫 was searched on 2026-11-21 (西門町 戲院, 豪華戲院, 來來百貨, 獅子林, 武昌街 電影街, 萬年大樓, 西門町 街景): it gave S28 and S29. Still no period photo of the cinema name signs, of 來來 in colour, or of 萬年 and 獅子林 before 2008.
- Two 國史館 press sets would likely settle the cinema signs and 萬年: 「春節期間電影街人潮洶湧」 1978-02-08 (典藏號 150-031700-0002-029) and the 萬年大樓 fire 1980-07-26 (150-031900-0003-028). 國家文化記憶庫 shows only their negative sleeves; the 國史館 online viewer (ahonline.drnh.gov.tw) hung on 系統處理中 for a guest and was not seen.
- The 106 「1950至2006年臺灣常民生活」 street photos matched by 西門町 街景 carry no captions and watermarked thumbnails; they were not opened one by one.
- The brush typefaces (Kaiti TC, Weibei TC, Xingkai TC, Yuanti TC) are macOS system fonts. On a device without them the signs fall back to 黑體; shipping a font file is `(unknown — needs CJ)`.
- The photo showcases and event markers (`photowall.js`, `places.js`) are interface, not street signs, and are unchanged.
