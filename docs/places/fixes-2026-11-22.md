# Weak-spot fixes, 2026-11-22: what each piece was built from

One builder, one piece at a time (CJ, 2026-11-22: "rebuild with one agent step by step in background"). Each line says what the piece rests on. Labels: **measured** (a file, command or page read in this pass), **inferred** (a reading of evidence or general knowledge of the type), **guess**. No reference photo was opened in this pass, so every look that is not in `docs/places/*.json` or `docs/signage/` is inferred at best.

## 西門町

- Exit 6 (`remake/lm/ximen-exit6.js`): escalator **measured** (a 2025 press line found by web search: 「搭乘捷運西門站6號出口手扶梯，一踏出捷運口即是西門町」); lift **inferred** (a travel guide says Exit 6 has one, no official page opened); the vaulted glass canopy, parapets and fascia **inferred** from the common look of 板南線 exits; no operator logo drawn.
- 天后宮 lions (`lm/ximen-lions.js`): the male-with-ball and female-with-cub rule and the red cloth **inferred** (general knowledge of temple lions); this temple's own pair not seen.
- Red House (`lm/ximen-redhouse.js`): face width and board position **measured** by raycast on the model; tenants (二樓劇場, 16工房, 創意市集) **inferred** from memory of the place; wording and colours of the board and banners **guess**.
- 刺青街, 美國街 (`lm/ximen-tattoo-lane.js`, `lm/ximen-america-lane.js`): that they are a covered tattoo lane and a graffiti and import-clothes lane **measured** (`docs/places/ximen.json`); every studio and shop name **guess** (invented, no real business named).
- 中華商場 (`lm/ximen-chunghwa.js`): eight blocks in a row joined by footbridges, and the shop names, **measured** (`docs/places/ximen.json`, `docs/signage/sources.md`); 生生皮鞋 rooftop neon **inferred** from memory; block numerals 2 孝 and 8 平 **inferred** from the 忠孝仁愛信義和平 order.
- Fan-meet booth and viewpoint 6: the dark shape was the booth's back wall, **measured** in a screenshot before and after.

- 永樂市場 front (`lm/dadao-yongle.js`): the floor uses (market, 布業商場, tailors, 大稻埕戲苑) **measured** (`docs/places/dadao.json`); 丸隆生魚行 **inferred** from memory; the arcade's look **guess**.

- Arcades (`lm/dadao-arcade.js`): couplets are stock New Year pairs (**inferred**); door-plate numbers **guess**.
